## Mechanics (read AGENTS.md, src/components/site/Markdown.tsx, src/content/schema.ts)
- Markdown guides: put `{{pick:product-id|Label|Why we picked it|https://source-url}}` on its own line (label, why and source optional). It renders a card only when the product earns. Maximum 4 pick directives in a guide, and `relatedProducts` (frontmatter) at most 4 ids; every pick id should also be in relatedProducts. TS guides use the `gear:` array / relatedProducts the way the existing ones do.
- The prose around each pick must read well on its own and must name the product naturally where it helps the reader decide: say who it suits, the specific job it does in THIS article's situation, what the maker page states (cite it), what it does not do, and what to check before buying. That is the sales push: concrete, situational, and honest. A pick should feel like the answer to the problem the paragraph just raised.
- Allowed: manufacturer specs read from the maker page or the catalog record in content/products.yml (read that record first; it holds specSource and what is a maker claim). Not allowed: invented tests, ratings, review counts, prices, "best-selling", "we tested", testimonials, urgency tricks, claims not in the catalog or a cited source. No new unsourced facts; any new fact needs a cited source and a line in docs/CLAIMS-<slug>.md. No "Best value" style labels unless a source supports it. Do not mention stock or availability.
- Never mention retired products. Retired ids (inactive, must not appear anywhere in guides, frontmatter, sources or prose as recommendations): saker-bomber-harness, orvis-embossed-sherpa-throw, akc-reunite-stainless-collar-tag, gunner-long-haul-bed, petmate-silicone-travel-bowl-duo, tractive-dog-6-xl, kong-ultimate-cargo-liner, kurgo-rsg-dog-first-aid-kit, kurgo-mud-dog-travel-shower, ruffwear-highlands-sleeping-bag, kurgo-heather-hammock, gunner-g1-kennel, sleepypod-clickit-range, kong-bench-seat-cover-hammock-xl, north-states-petyard-passage-8-panel, midwest-lifestages-dog-crate, baskerville-ultra-muzzle, amk-me-and-my-dog, kurgo-dog-first-aid-kit. Remove their references and rewrite the surrounding text so it still makes sense (a research table may still describe a non-earning product only when a test result or safety fact needs it, with no label or recommendation language; prefer removing).
- House rules: no em dashes style issues beyond the repo's existing tone (read two published guides first), plain language, one idea per paragraph, no filler, no AI-disclosure repeats, never say a stay is verified. Keep sources lists accurate; add the maker pages you cite. Update `lastReviewed` to 2026-11-19 when you change a guide, and keep readingMinutes within 1 of the real length.

## Authenticity gate
For each guide decide: INTEGRATED (products fit and you wrote them in), PARTIAL, or NO-FIT. NO-FIT means every plausible product would be a stretch for the reader of that guide (a guide about a legal or policy question where gear is not the reader's problem). Do not force it: leave that guide's prose unchanged apart from retired-id cleanup and report NO-FIT with a one-line reason and what you considered. The lead will decide to remove it.

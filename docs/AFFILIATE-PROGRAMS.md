# Affiliate Programs Research (Paw Passage)

Researched 2026-11-17. Written for the site owner (non-technical) and a future buyer. "NOT CONFIRMED" means I could not verify it on an official page or in an official search result. Quotes are exact; the page each came from is in the Sources list. Terms change often: re-check each page the day you apply.

Plain-English rule of thumb: only show a link for a program you have been APPROVED in. Rates and cookie lengths below are only given when an official page states them.

## 1. Amazon Associates (US)

**Who can join and approval.** The Operating Agreement covers "Any person or entity that participates or attempts to participate in our associate marketing program" and requires that "you are lawfully able to enter into contracts (e.g. you are not a minor or otherwise legally prevented from contracting)". The Participation Requirements say: "You must identify your Site(s) in your application" and "Your Site(s) must contain original content and be publicly available". Also: "We will evaluate your application and notify you of its acceptance or rejection." A search-result summary of Amazon's help pages mentions a rule of thumb of "at least 10 posts" and content "generally within the last 60 days"; I did not see that on a page I opened, so treat it as PROBABLE, not confirmed.

**The sales requirement (the big one).** Amazon's Application Review page says the team will "check your application once you've driven qualified sales" and "(we require at least three within the first 180 days)"; "personal orders do not qualify." The Account Dormancy page says: "Once you've applied, you have 180 days to refer a sale through one of your Associates links." and "Your application can be withdrawn if there were not enough qualifying sales for 180 days after your application was submitted."

**If you miss it.** "We're unable to reinstate your account or Associates ID after it's been rejected." The Application Review page adds: "Unfortunately, we aren't able to reassess an application once it's been rejected." Whether you may simply re-apply is unclear (Amazon's own pages conflict): NOT CONFIRMED. Practical meaning: the 180-day clock starts when you apply, so apply when the site is live with real content and visitors.

**Making links.** Special Links must use "the special 'tagged' link formats we provide" and "the Associates ID we have assigned to you." Amazon's help text (search result) says SiteStripe is the toolbar at the top of Amazon pages when you are signed in to Associates Central ("Tools" then "SiteStripe"); the tag appears in the link as `&tag=yourid-20`. Extra tracking IDs are optional (limit 100, per search result).

**Required disclosure wording (Operating Agreement, Section 5):** "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." The agreement also bars other public statements about the program without permission, so use this sentence exactly, near Amazon links. Participation Requirements add that you must disclose "on your Site accurately and adequately".

**Rules that matter for this site (Participation Requirements / Policies):**
- Prices: "your Site may only show prices and availability if" the link is Amazon-served or the data comes from Amazon's approved API. Our site shows no prices: compliant. Never type a price by hand.
- Cloaking/shorteners: "You will not cloak, hide, spoof, or otherwise obscure the URL of your Site containing Special Links." Shorteners are barred when "it is unclear that you are linking to an Amazon Site". Do not use bit.ly-style links or redirect tools that hide Amazon.
- Email/messages: allowed only if "such communications are solicited (i.e., opted into by the receiving customer)". Do not email links to cold lists.
- Own site only: "All Special Links must be accessed directly from your Site." Do not put tagged links on other sites or social profiles not listed in the application. Print, mail and oral use are barred.
- Cookie: the session ends when "24 hours elapse from that click"; add-to-cart purchases count if completed "no later than 89 days" after the click. (No 90-day cookie.)
- Prohibited sites include those that "promote or contain sexually explicit or obscene materials" or "promote or undertake illegal activities". Reviews must not be paid for.
- Our `rel="sponsored nofollow"` is fine; nothing I found forbids it.

**Transfer to a buyer.** Section 12 of the Operating Agreement: "You may not assign this Agreement, by operation of law or otherwise, without our express prior written approval." So the account is NOT freely transferable. No Amazon process for selling an account was found: NOT CONFIRMED. Plan on the buyer applying for their own account (and starting their own 180-day clock), then swapping the tag. Note also "Program Content" cannot be sold or transferred.

**Before launch.** (1) Own the live site; list it in the application. (2) Publish real original articles first. (3) Provide tax information when asked ("If we request tax information from you and you do not provide it to us, we reserve the right" to hold commissions). Exact payment/identity steps: NOT CONFIRMED. (4) Put the disclosure sentence on the site. (5) Make at least 3 separate qualifying orders (not your own) inside 180 days.

## 6. FTC disclosure rules

From "Disclosures 101" (FTC): make sure people "see and understand" the disclosure; "The disclosure should be placed with the endorsement message itself." Disclosures on an "ABOUT ME or profile page" or behind a "MORE" link are easily missed. "Don't mix your disclosure into a group of hashtags or links." Avoid vague shorthand ("sp", "spon", "collab").

From the FTC's Endorsement Guides Q&A (affiliate links): "The closer the disclosure is to your recommendation, the better." First-person example: "I get commissions for purchases made through links in this post." "Consumers might not understand that 'affiliate link' means that the person placing the link is getting paid." "'Paid link' right next to an affiliate link should be an adequate disclosure." "A 'buy now' button would not be adequate." "'Commissionable link' is probably not a clear disclosure." A footer-style hyperlink is weak: "A hyperlinked disclosure like that is easily avoidable, meaning that the disclosure is not clear and conspicuous."

Do: put a plain sentence above the first product pick on every article, e.g. "I earn a commission if you buy through links on this page, at no extra cost to you." plus Amazon's exact sentence. Do not: rely only on a separate disclosure page, bury it at the page bottom, or say only "affiliate link". Also do not say or imply you tested products (the FTC page warns against discussing "a product you haven't tried"). "Plain language" is confirmed by the FTC; whether "I" vs "we" matters is not addressed on the pages I read (the FTC's example uses "I").

## 7. Suggested order of applying (new site, no traffic)

1. **Awin** (free; its only stated requirement is "a means of linking to an advertiser"; approval "typically" within 24 hours). Then ask for Fi (base 10%), Petmate/Vittles Vault (base 10%) and, if wanted, Booking.com.
2. **Expedia Group Travel Creator Program** (free, "Apply in minutes", no audience size stated) for Vrbo links. Do this before adding stay links.
3. **AvantLink** for Adventure Medical Kits (official page invites "Content Partners"; no traffic minimum is shown).
4. **Amazon Associates** once the site is live with real articles and some visitors, because the 180-day / 3-sale clock starts at application and a failed account "cannot be reinstated". It covers the most products on this list (about 20 of 27 found).
5. **Impact and others needing proof of audience:** Chewy (quality review), Tractive (pet business required), Kurgo (possible site-age minimum: NOT CONFIRMED), Orvis (Rakuten, status NOT CONFIRMED). Apply after a few months of traffic.
6. Skip: Airbnb (closed), ShareASale (closed), GUNNER and Hipcamp (influencer/ambassador, not open affiliate).

## 8. Risks and unknowns

- Amazon: whether a rejected applicant can re-apply; exact tax/payment steps; any buyer-account transfer route. Assume none.
- Amazon links to out-of-stock items ("Currently unavailable") may earn nothing.
- Commission rates for Kurgo, Orvis, Booking.com, Chewy US, Petco, PetSmart, BringFido, Hipcamp: NOT CONFIRMED from an official page.
- Brands may reject a site with no traffic; approvals are never guaranteed, and a program can close at any time (Airbnb, ShareASale did).
- No brand affiliate program found for Sleepypod, Lucky Duck, Company of Animals, Springland, MidWest, North States, Carlson, AKC Reunite: ask each by email, or rely on Amazon.
- Prices change: the site shows none, so nothing to maintain, but never add them (Amazon rule).
- Tractive may decline a general travel site.
- Selling the site: affiliate accounts are personal; the buyer must apply fresh. Budget weeks of "no earning links".
- Keep `rel="sponsored nofollow"` and the disclosure on every page with links; a missing disclosure risks both FTC and program termination.

## Top 5 issues (most important first)
1. The page does not answer "how much" in the first screen.
   - Where: the opening paragraphs and "## The short answer".
   - What is wrong: The title and the primary keyword ("how much does it cost to take a dog on vacation") ask for a cost, but the first screen gives a method (four piles) and the sentence "we skip those tables here". The actual range ($20 to $50 a night, $50 to $400 flat or per pet, one manager "up to $1,000") first appears in "## The pet fee line, in our dataset", well down the page.
   - Exact fix: Edit 1 (add the range to the short answer).
2. Heading contradicts its table, and the dataset sentence is miscounted.
   - Where: "## Two quotes, side by side (hypothetical)" (the table has Rental A, B and C) and "Twenty-five state a fee. Three of those say $0 (Sliding Rock Cabins, Twiddy & Company and Hapey Cabin Rentals), and 13 give no amount."
   - What is wrong: "Two quotes" heads a three-quote table, and the sibling no-pet-fee guide says 22 charge, three are $0 and 13 give no amount. As written, "Three of those... and 13 give no amount" reads as if 13 is inside the 25, then the next paragraph says "Among the 22 that charge". The numbers add up (25 + 13 = 38) but the sentence does not say so.
   - Exact fix: Edits 2 and 3.
3. The gear section is bolted on and weakly tied to the topic.
   - Where: "## Small gear to pack" (towel, mat, bed, kibble bin).
   - What is wrong: The intro admits gear is not in the totals and "none of it changes what a host or platform may charge". Fine, but the IRIS airtight kibble container has no link to rental cost, cleaning fees or damage; two items are both Soggy Doggy entry-mess products (towel and mat) with overlapping jobs; and there is no "if you buy one" line or any "who can skip it". The reader who came for cost advice sees four products and no shortcut.
   - Exact fix: Edits 5 and 6.
4. No path to action after the comparison and thin sibling links.
   - Where: "## How to compare two quotes" and "## Next step".
   - What is wrong: The reader is told to compare quotes and add tasks to the checklist, but there is no link to find homes or to the weight rule that can eliminate a rental ("a rental that is $50 cheaper and caps dogs below yours is not an option" has no link to the weight guide). The related-guides block shows only three cards.
   - Exact fix: Edits 7 and 8.
5. Title tag and H1 do not carry the main search phrase.
   - Where: seoTitle "What a Dog Really Costs on a Rental Trip" and H1 "What a Dog Really Costs on a Vacation Rental".
   - What is wrong: The likely query is "how much does it cost to take a dog to a vacation rental" or "pet fee total cost". "Really costs" is a good hook but contains no "how much" or "pet fee", and the description lacks "how much".
   - Exact fix: Edit 4.

## What works well
- The three-pile model (definite cost, refundable cash, contingent charges) and the three-quote table that separates "definite cost" from "cash needed up front" is a real decision tool; the "ranking can flip with a second dog" example is correct and instructive. Nothing in the competitor set offers it.
- Honest framing everywhere: the quotes are labelled hypothetical and "not prices from any listing"; Cabin Rentals of Georgia's ambiguous $75 is flagged as "we read it as charged once"; platform claims are tied to Airbnb and Vrbo help pages with numbered citations.
- Clean structure for scanning: headings for cleaning, deposits, service fees, taxes and extras; a numbered six-step comparison list; an open-questions table. All tables fit at 390 px.

## 3. Apply now or wait for traffic? (JUDGMENT, based on official statements)

Official facts that drive this: Amazon starts a 180-day, 3-sale clock at application and finalizes a rejection; AvantLink rejects most applicants and wants a verified, non-thin site; Awin and Impact say advertisers decide on their own criteria and no traffic minimum is stated on the pages read; Impact lists "limited reach or activity" as a decline reason and says "grow your audience, then reapply". I found no official site-age minimum for any program. I did not find any statement that approval at one program is a prerequisite for another; do not tell Chewy otherwise.

| Program | Recommendation | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Awin: Fi, Petmate | Apply after deploy | Free, per-advertiser, rejection does not touch the Awin account; products exist on the hub |
| Ascend: Kurgo | Apply after deploy | Best brand fit; rate and rules are in a guide to read first |
| BringFido | Apply after deploy, after reading the agreement | Natural stays fit; revocable at their discretion per snippet |
| Impact: Tractive | Apply after deploy | Relevance test; products are on the hub |
| Amazon | Wait for first organic or Pinterest visits, then apply (target: weeks, not days) | 3 sales in 180 days or the application is withdrawn; a rejection cannot be reassessed. Applying with no traffic wastes the one shot (judgment) |
| AvantLink / AMK | Wait (about 2 to 3 months of live content, JUDGMENT) | Close to 80% denial rate on its FAQ; thin or new sites are the likely casualty |
| PetSafe | Wait | Wants "strong online presence and loyal following"; competing-brand clause |
| Chewy | Ask first; do not resubmit | Impact: "Grow your audience, then reapply"; reapply rules unknown |
| Wild One, Booking.com, Petco, Gunner, Ruffwear, Sleepypod, REI, Backcountry, Orvis | Not now | No product on site, or terms unreadable |

Order is a judgment, not a rule: cheap, per-advertiser, reversible applications first; one-shot applications (Amazon) after traffic exists.

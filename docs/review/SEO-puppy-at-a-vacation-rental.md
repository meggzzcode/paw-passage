No keyword-volume tool; demand below is an ESTIMATE from WebSearch result pages and the brief's Search Console notes.

- **Primary keyword:** vacation rental with a puppy (4 words, clear planning intent). Rejected: "traveling with a puppy" (too broad, owned by AKC/Cornell/VCA-type pages) and the old 6-word "traveling with a puppy vacation rental".

- **People Also Ask / related questions (from result pages and a second "puppy to an airbnb" search):** can you take a puppy to an Airbnb; do vacation rentals allow puppies / how old must a dog be; how much are pet fees and does damage cost extra; how to puppy-proof a rental; what to pack for a puppy trip. The article answers all of these (FAQ now has the Airbnb question).
- **Demand ESTIMATE: medium.** Mainstream publishers (AARP, Booking.com, AKC, Cornell) fill the general results, which shows demand, but none targets "puppy + rental age rule", so a narrow, specific page can compete. Difficulty medium for the broad phrase, low for the age-rule angle.

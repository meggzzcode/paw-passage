## Top 5 issues (most important first)
1. **SEO fit (score 3).** Where: the title tag "Dog-Friendly Rental Checklist: 8 Questions" and `research.primaryKeyword` "pet friendly vacation rental checklist". What is wrong: the title tag drops both "pet-friendly" and "vacation" from the keyword the article was researched for, while the H1 says "Pet-Friendly Vacation Rental". Searchers for "pet friendly vacation rental checklist" see a title that does not echo the query, and "8 Questions" conflicts with the body, which has far more than 8 tick boxes. Fix: set `seoTitle` to "Pet-Friendly Vacation Rental Checklist: 8 Gates" (47 chars) and keep the meta description, adding "checklist" once: "A pet-friendly vacation rental checklist: eight gates to clear before you pay, covering written approval, total price, unattended-dog rules, cancellation and an emergency vet." (under 170, trim if needed to 160).
2. **Intro buries the answer.** Where: "You found a cabin with a hot tub and a" and the paragraph after it. What is wrong: the first paragraph ends on a side link ("Pool and hot tub access is its own question") and the second paragraph ends on a dangling sentence, "If a listing already feels off, question the vague wording before you book.", that goes nowhere. On mobile the 8-item gate only appears after two paragraphs of links and the hero image. Fix: keep the first paragraph through "...before you paid. Work top to bottom, and stop at the first line you cannot answer." Move the pool link into the "The physical stay" section. Delete the dangling sentence and merge the checklist-tool and fees-guide links into one sentence: "Tick it off on your phone in the [interactive checklist](/tools/checklist); the [pet fees guide](/guides/how-vacation-rental-pet-fees-work) shows how the total is built."
3. **"8 questions" versus about 35 tick boxes.** Where: "The 8-item booking gate" followed by Permission, Price, House rules, Physical stay, Local rules, Service animals, Before you leave. What is wrong: the promise (title tag, pin, H2) is eight questions, but each later section has 2 to 6 more checkboxes, so a reader cannot tell which are the eight deal-breakers and which are details. "An unanswered preference can wait. An unanswered deal-breaker cannot." is the right idea but is never mapped. Fix: label each H2 with its gate number (for example "Permission (gate 1)", "Price (gate 2)", "House rules (gate 3)", "The physical stay (gates 4 and 5)", "Local rules and an emergency plan (gates 7 and 8)", "Cancellation (gate 6)"), and add one line under the gate list: "Everything below is detail for these eight; the first box in each section is the one that blocks a booking." Split "Service animals and cancellation" into two H2s so cancellation is findable from the gate.
4. **Gear section placement and framing.** Where: "Gear that matches the checklist", after "Before you leave". What is wrong: four cards all labelled "Our pick" sit after the reader has finished; the section reads as an add-on, and the pen card duplicates the dedicated pen guide while the crate, bed and gate are each justified by one line. No card says who should skip it. The card text itself is accurate to the catalog. Fix: add one sentence at the start: "Most stays need none of this; buy only for a line you flagged, and skip anything the host already provides." Change the four card labels from "Our pick" to the line they answer: "For the crate line", "For the sofas line", "For the fence line", "For the unattended line". Move the section above "How sure we are" is already the case; keep it there but add a one-line "Skip it if" to each paragraph (crate: skip if the host provides a crate your dog fits; bed: skip if furniture is allowed; pen: skip without written host permission; gate: skip if the dog can jump 26 in).
5. **Misleading anchor and over-linking.** Where: "Choosing a platform? See [Airbnb vs Vrbo with a dog]" at the close. What is wrong: the link goes to /guides/how-vacation-rental-pet-fees-work, which is a fees guide, not an Airbnb versus Vrbo comparison, and that same page is linked four or five times in the article (intro, Price, closing twice). Fix: replace the sentence with "Choosing a platform? Pet-fee terms differ by platform; see [how pet fees actually work](/guides/how-vacation-rental-pet-fees-work)." and remove the second "Next step: read our pet fees guide" link, keeping "then save your answers in the [checklist tool](/tools/checklist)."

## What works well
- The one-message template and the "bookable / pending / reject" rule turn the checklist into a decision, which most competitor checklists in the research block do not do.
- Primary-source anchoring: Vrbo house rules, Airbnb pet-fee and off-platform pages, AVMA and ADA are cited at the line where a reader would doubt a claim, and the "38 managers, convenience sample, read 2026-11-18" statement is honest about scope.
- The fee example (per night per pet versus per stay) shows why the unit matters and attributes both figures to the managers with a date; the research-not-testing stance is stated once in "How sure we are" rather than repeated.

## Buyer walkthrough
1. Lands from a search for a pet-friendly rental checklist, sees the title, dek, hero and disclosure box, then two intro paragraphs of links before the gate.
2. Works through the gate and the sections; the decision moment is "bookable / pending / reject" after "One message to send".
3. Reaches "Gear that matches the checklist" last; each card is tied to a checklist line, with a "See it at Amazon" button, a "Source" link and a "Details and specs" link.
4. Friction: product names in the prose (iCrate, Loft Wander) link to the maker page, not to the on-site gear entry, and the four cards are all labelled "Our pick" with no "skip it if" guidance.
5. Next step: the closing links go to the fees guide and the checklist tool; related guides sit in "Keep reading". No dead end.

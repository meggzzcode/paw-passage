## Top 5 issues (most important first)

1. **The two "buy first" lists contradict each other, and the closing list names products that do not exist on the page.**
   Where: "What to buy first" ("A lid-tight food container, a drying towel and an entry mat come first; a cooling pad only if...") versus the bullet "What to buy:" under "The short answer" ("a lid-tight food container, a washable drying towel, a roll-up bed that is clearly the dog's, and a clip-on light for dark toilet trips").
   What is wrong: the short answer says bed and light, the close says entry mat and cooling pad. There is no entry-mat card and no cooling-pad card (the Kurgo bed and the Beacon, which the short answer promises, are missing from the close). A reader who scrolls to the end gets a different shopping list from the one at the top and cannot click anything for two of the four items named. This is the single most visible buyer-trust break in the article.
   Fix: make the close match the card set. Replace the first sentence of "What to buy first" with the text in edit 1 below, and replace "Buying checklist" item 6 so it reads as one consistent four-item list.

2. **Reader flow: the packing list is scattered and the product-heavy section is mis-filed.**
   Where: heading "Protecting the rental: bed, throw and towels" (about 4000 px tall on desktop, 1500 px of which is two product cards, the Beacon paragraph and a cooling-pad paragraph).
   What is wrong: this section is meant to be about furniture and floors, but it also holds "Hot weather" (cooling pad) and "Dark toilet trips" (the Beacon light), neither of which is rental protection. A reader looking for "what light do I bring" has to know to look under a heading about throws and towels. The towel and bed cards are also stacked back to back with no prose between them, so the second card reads as an unexplained advert. The article never gives one scannable "pack this" list, even though the dek and the title promise a packing list: the real list is spread over 13 sections and 11 minutes, and the tool link (`/tools/packing-list`) is the only place the reader can tick items off.
   Fix: split into two headings (edits 3 and 4) and add a compact one-screen "Pack list at a glance" (edit 5) right after "The short answer".

3. **Hero image and Pinterest pin show a hiking-pack dog at a mountain lake, not a cabin.**
   Where: hero (alt "A white dog on a leash wearing a grey and charcoal saddlebag-style pack, standing on a boulder beside a mountain lake") and `public/pins/dog-friendly-cabin-packing-list.jpg`.
   What is wrong: a reader landing from Pinterest for "what to pack for a dog at a cabin" sees a trail photo, and the same photo appears again as a related card on the hiking-gear guide, so the image reads as hiking, not packing. Nothing in the article tells the reader to buy a dog pack. On desktop the hero also consumes the whole first viewport below the dek, so the first body sentence is not visible until after one scroll; on mobile the short answer starts at about 1330 px, about two screens down.
   Fix: swap the hero and pin for a cabin-interior or porch-with-bags image already in the library (see edit 14), or at minimum reword the alt text so it does not promise a cabin; and move the "Pack list at a glance" so it is the first thing under the disclosure banner on mobile.

4. **Internal linking: no link to the two sibling guides that cover the water and trail items this list leaves out.**
   Where: the whole article; `relatedGuides` lists fenced yard, pet-friendly checklist, two-dog, NYE, Christmas week only. The text sends the reader to park and trail rules ("Check the rules for each park and trail") and to "boots" ("Trial anything new, such as boots...") without linking to `/guides/dog-hiking-gear-for-a-cabin-vacation`, and the competitor set it cites includes life jackets and booties. Meanwhile both sibling guides link back to this one, so the cluster is one-way.
   What is wrong: the highest-intent reader (a dog owner with a cabin on a lake or trailhead) dead-ends. This also wastes the best internal-link opportunity for the hiking and life-jacket pages.
   Fix: edits 8 and 9 (in-text links) and add both slugs to `relatedGuides` (edit 10). Also the text "Georgia cabin-vetting guide" links to `/guides/vacation-rental-secure-fenced-yard`, the same URL as the earlier "secure fenced-yard guide", and the guide is not Georgia-specific in its slug; rename the anchor (edit 11).

5. **Repetition and awkward wording that make the buying advice read as hedging.**
   Where: the maker-has-no-bear-claim statement appears five times: "The short answer" (bullet 4), "Buying checklist" item 1, the "Storage" paragraph, the Iris pick card and the FAQ answer "Can I leave dog food on the cabin deck in a sealed bin?". Also the sentence "Keep pet food, treats, bowls, and rubbish indoors or as the property and park direct." (the clause "or as the property and park direct" is unclear), and every pick card eyebrow is the generic "Our pick".
   What is wrong: the same caveat five times trains the reader to skim it; the generic "Our pick" label says nothing about who the product is for or who should skip it, and it clashes with the honest "researched, not tested" stance elsewhere because "pick" with no reason reads as a ranking.
   Fix: say it fully once (Storage paragraph and card), cut it to a short pointer elsewhere (edits 2, 6, 7), and replace each "Our pick" eyebrow with a one-line job-to-be-done (edit 12).

## What works well
- The bear-proof section is the best in the competitive set: it separates "tidy kibble bin" from bear-resistant storage, cites NPS and BearWise, and the Iris pick card admits the maker makes no bear-resistance claim (matches the catalog field `manufacturerClaimsVsIndependentTesting`).
- It covers real remote-cabin hazards other cabin lists skip, with sources: de-winterized chemicals and antifreeze in toilets, wood-stove clearance and ash, the stair-gate warning ("Never use a pressure-mounted gate at the top of stairs"), and phone numbers with the "a consultation fee may apply" caveat.
- Product specs on the page match the catalog exactly (Kurgo sizes and care, Soggy Doggy 31 x 14 in and care, Beacon modes, 20 h, 1.5 h and 1 m / 30 min figures are all labelled as maker figures), and the disclosure sits at the top of the body above the first link.

## Buyer walkthrough
1. Lands from search or the pin, sees a dog with a trail pack, reads the dek ("muddy entrances, unfamiliar stairs, quiet nights") and scrolls past the hero to "The short answer".
2. The short answer says to buy four things; the "Buying checklist" repeats them with no links, so the reader keeps scrolling to find them.
3. The Iris bin card sits about 40% down under Food; the towel and bed cards come at about 70% down, then the Beacon card, and the first-aid kit and tick tool have no product.
4. At "What to buy first" the reader is told to buy an entry mat and maybe a cooling pad, neither of which has a card; friction and a lost click.
5. Ends at the packing-list tool, the checklist and /stays; there is no pointer to hiking gear or life jackets, and no /gear hub link before "Gear for this trip".

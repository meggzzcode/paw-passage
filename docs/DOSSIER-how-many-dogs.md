### 2f. Scripts and documents that worked

- Photo of the listing's pet policy before booking: only REPORTED in a search summary.

### Nov 20 2026 "Pets" house-rule wording (the verification the brief asked for)
- Airbnb's own page: NOT found. The 3623 page does not mention a Pets house rule, and states only that the house-rules section of the listing says whether you can bring a pet.

It cites no Airbnb page for pets. rentalscaleup.com lists "Pets" among seven standard rules and gives no count wording. Announcement date 20 Oct 2026 and effective date 20 Nov 2026 for new bookings are consistent across the sources the search returned (REPORTED).
- Verdict: usable as "third-party explainers say", not as Airbnb wording. NOT CONFIRMED on an Airbnb page.

### Contradictions
- Airbnb gives guests a 0-5 declaration and hosts a 1-5 cap in the host guide. The guest page does not say whether a host with a limit of 2 can stop a guest declaring 3 (NOT CONFIRMED).
- Vrbo help describes a size/type note and no count field; the Vrbo listing page we read stated "limit 2 total" in an FAQ (SEEN), so hosts put counts in free text.
- Twiddy: pets free and 2 max; third-party pet-travel sites say "no weight restriction" (REPORTED). Twiddy's own page sets no weight limit but says homes may restrict "number, size, or type".
- Tokeet's numbered scenario says eight dogs but lists six (SEEN). Do not repeat the "eight".

## 7. COMPILED FINDINGS FOR THE WRITER

### Ten useful insights
1. Airbnb has two numbers: guests declare 0-5 pets (3623, SEEN), hosts set a limit from 1 to 5 (hosting guide, updated 8 Jan 2025, SEEN). Nobody ranking cites both.
2. Airbnb's fee options are four, and the fee can't exceed the nightly base rate (SEEN). So a guest with 3 dogs pays one flat fee or three, depending on the host's choice. Read the total at checkout.
3. Vrbo help shows a "Pets allowed" rule with a note field of 50 characters; counts live in free text (SEEN), so the listing FAQ and house rules both need reading.
4. Managers set hard, published caps: Twiddy 2, Brindley 2 at 60 lb each, My Vacation Haven 2, AvantStay "up to two" (SEEN). Table in section 4 is a number a reader cannot easily assemble.
5. Fee structures are not comparable per dog: $250 first and $100 second dog (My Vacation Haven), $50/night/dog (CRG), flat per reservation (Brindley). Show the same 3-night stay worked three ways, using only quoted figures.
6. Unannounced dogs cost money by policy: CRG doubles pet fees, Shiny Apple lists $200 (REPORTED), Brindley and Twiddy allow eviction/termination (SEEN).
7. Some managers use a default but say "unless advertised", so a 3-dog home exists (Shiny Apple up to 3, REPORTED; Camino, CA listing 3 small, REPORTED). Filter by listing text.
8. Hosts' own forum view: count is a weak predictor of mess (item 1), yet a 3-dog stay is the cited cautionary tale (item 4). Hosts decide on mixed anecdotes, insurance and local rules.
9. Service-animal claims appear in host stories about extra dogs (REPORTED). Policies read say service animals are free and must be accepted; none read says whether they count toward a cap. State exactly that.
10. Nov 20 2026 pets rule: only third parties describe a "how many" option (guestable, 2 Nov 2026). Call it unconfirmed.

### Five things that beat what ranks
1. A sourced table of manager limits and fees with URLs (section 4).
2. A worked fee example for 1, 2 and 3 dogs under each fee model, from quoted numbers only.
3. The two-number explanation (guest declares 0-5; host limit 1-5) with citations.
4. A copy-paste message for a 3+ dog request, plus a "what to get in writing" checklist.
5. An honest "NOT CONFIRMED" box (service animals vs cap, Airbnb override, local caps).

### Solid enough to state
Airbnb 0-5 declaration and four fee models, cap at nightly base rate, service animals free (3623). Hosts can limit 1-5 (hosting guide). Twiddy 2 default, Brindley 2 and 60 lb each, CRG fees and 35 lb, My Vacation Haven $250/$100, Vacasa varies per home and per dog per night. Vrbo "Pets allowed" rule with 50-character note. ADA hotel cleaning-fee wording, not for rentals.

### Hedge
"Most listings allow one or two dogs" (our table is a handful of managers; attribute to "the managers we read"). Typical 50 lb limit (REPORTED, listing snippets). Hotel limits (REPORTED). The Nov 20 2026 Pets rule. Any claim that hosts commonly bend (single anecdote). Marriott Homes and Villas $150 per dog and the Blue Ridge listings (search summaries).

### Leave out
Fee averages or percentages of hosts charging (unsourced elsewhere). "Eight dogs" (Tokeet's own figures disagree). Any claim that city dog caps bind guests. Any legal statement that a host must accept a service animal beyond quoting Airbnb and ADA wording. Vrbo "does not charge per pet" (single host-community comment, REPORTED).

### Recommended outline (question H2s)
1. How many dogs can you bring to an Airbnb or vacation rental? (no platform-wide number; Airbnb 0-5 and 1-5; ask in writing)
2. How does Airbnb count pets, and what does the host see?
3. How does Vrbo handle the number of pets?
4. What do rental managers actually allow? (table, section 4)
5. Is there a weight limit for dogs in a vacation rental?
6. What does an extra dog cost? (worked fee example, four Airbnb models plus manager shapes)
7. What happens if you bring more dogs than the listing allows? (policy wording first; host anecdotes labelled)
8. How do you ask for 3 or more dogs? (script, what to get in writing, what hosts worry about: insurance, noise, hair)
9. Do puppies and service animals count toward the limit?
10. Is a hotel easier with several dogs? (hedged until brand pages are read)
11. What changes on November 20, 2026? (only if verified; otherwise one hedged sentence)

Final note: no Airbnb or Vrbo page states what counts toward a limit, so the closing FAQ should say "ask the host and keep it in writing".

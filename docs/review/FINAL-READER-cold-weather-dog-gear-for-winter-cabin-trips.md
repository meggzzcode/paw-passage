# Reader review: cold-weather-dog-gear-for-winter-cabin-trips
Method: Playwright Chromium at 390 and 1280 px, screenshots, text and link audit. Page height 14,199 px at 390 (about 2,500 words).

## Role 1: first-time search visitor
- First screen (390): title, subhead ("Vets and brands give four different cold limits and no rating for your dog..."), hero photo, buttons, disclosure. Answer paragraph and "At a glance: our picks" are one scroll down. The first screen promises a method, not a pick; a visitor wanting "does my dog need a coat" gets it in the first paragraph below the box: good.
- Hero photo shows a dog in a pink puffer coat, but the page says "we feature no such coat" and sells fleeces; mild mismatch between image and recommendation.
- The page is a coat-and-boots article with a towel and a car section bolted on. "The car and the cabin" (antifreeze, do not leave a dog in the car) is thin, and "Keeping a rental clean and dry" mostly serves the towel product.

## Role 2: mobile traveler planning a trip
- Table "How cold is too cold, by dog type" has only two rows and is not needed: it renders at 390 as two narrow columns with the first column carrying long parentheticals ("Short or thin coat, lean build (ASPCA suggests a coat or sweater with a high collar; thin dogs get cold faster because body fat insulates)") making a tall, ragged cell, and a "Sources: PetMD, Cornell, ASPCA, Outward Hound" line under it. No horizontal overflow, but a bullet list would read better. The Cornell Huskies/Malamute dogs are stated in prose and not in the table, so the table is incomplete as a "by dog type" device.
- Repeated answers: "neither fleece is a shell, and we feature no such coat: shop for an insulated, windproof..." (At a glance) is restated in "A fleece suits a short walk... it is a layer, not a shell" and in "Treat it as a dry-weather layer". The ASPCA line "if it's too cold for you, it's probably too cold for your pet" appears in the cold-limits paragraph and the Christmas guide. "Skip boots on clear, dry paths" appears in At a glance and "Skip them if your paths are clear" in the boots section. Sizing contradiction explained once, then again for the vest ("same size-up or size-down wording").
- "Wiping is the habit that covers every dog." and "Do this at home, not on the cabin porch in the dark." are fine, human lines. "Our routine, not from a source:" is a nice honest touch.
- Product cards (4): each follows a paragraph that justifies it, and includes "Skip it" guidance. The Soggy Doggy towel is the weakest: a generic towel appears as a pick "for every dog" with no cold-trip reason beyond "snow turns into water"; it reads as a filler product. Same triple repetition of specs (paragraph, chip, "Why we picked it") as the other guides. The vest card interrupts the fleece/vest comparison but it is placed after its paragraph, so no instruction is cut.
- Last paragraph "Before you go, measure paws and chest, add the towel to the packing list tool, look over the winter travel gear page, and run the pre-booking checklist..." is a link dump in one sentence.

## Role 3: skeptical reader
- Strong evidence handling: four temperature thresholds named by source (45/40/32/20 F), the PetMD limits on sweaters, and the Ruffwear sizing contradiction. "What we could not confirm" is actually specific.
- Voice: some stock/hedging: "This is not veterinary advice; ask your vet", "Neither the sources nor the maker pages we read give a safe temperature..." repeated in lead, in "How cold" and in "What we could not confirm". Lead and the last section answer the same thing.
- Citations: mix of inline publisher links and trailing parentheticals "(Cornell Vet, PetMD)" and "(NBC News Select)"; a parenthetical `(Cornell Vet)` is attached to a sentence whose claim is from a Chewy veterinarian quoted by NBC Select ("a Chewy veterinarian quoted by NBC Select says not to leave a dog in a car with the heat off (Cornell Vet)") which looks mis-attributed: the sentence cites Cornell for an NBC-quoted claim.
- Disclosure: single top notice with Amazon sentence; no per-card labels. 12 Amazon links (4 products x 3), all tagged and rel sponsored. Fine.
- Safety: "if size between, size down" for boots per the maker is attributed. Petroleum jelly suggestion is attributed to ASPCA with "vet-approved"; acceptable. Nothing dangerous.
- Perceived length: long (about 9 screens). The sources list (12) is collapsed.

## Observable defects
1. Two-row table is unnecessary and awkward at 390 (tall first column).
2. Wet-snow "no shell" caveat and "no sources give a number" repeated in lead, body and "What we could not confirm".
3. Citation mismatch in the car paragraph: Chewy vet via NBC Select followed by "(Cornell Vet)".
4. Soggy Doggy towel card has weak connection to the cold-weather question; "Before you go" paragraph is a five-link run-on.
5. Hero photo (pink puffer coat) conflicts with "we feature no such coat".
6. Spec repetition across paragraph, chips and "Why we picked it".

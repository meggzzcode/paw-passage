## E. Questions to answer before any asset ships

1. Lovable: where did the six concept images come from? Their embedded credentials say "gpt-image" and AI-generated
   (trainedAlgorithmicMedia). Which account or service and which plan generated them, and on whose behalf?
2. Lovable and owner: what licence and commercial-use terms apply to those outputs (the generator's terms for that account), and
   may they appear on a monetised site and in marketing material?
3. Is there an obligation or desire to label them as AI-generated (site copy, alt text, IMAGE-CREDITS.md)? The site currently
   presents CC0 real photos for keepsakes; a mix needs an honest note.
4. Do any images depict real, recognizable people or real dogs/brands/places? If any person is shown, is a release needed or must
   it be replaced?
5. Will the final design keep these images, regenerate them, or use licensed or original photography instead? Provide a
   proof for whichever path (receipt, file page or generator terms).
6. Fonts: confirm the final font list and fonts source; supply (or let us download) official font files and their OFL text so
   they can be self-hosted. Confirm none is paid.
7. Illustrations (`then-and-now`, `seasonal`, `camping-patch`): who made them, and with what tool?
8. Is the C2PA content credential to be kept on shipped images (strip or keep)? If stripped, still document the origin here.

## F. How to mark an item VERIFIED

Add the proof next to the row: a licence text in the repo, a Wikimedia or other file page URL with date checked, a generator
terms URL with date and account, or a signed owner statement saved outside the repo (record only its existence, no secrets).
Then change Status to VERIFIED and add the date and the person who confirmed.

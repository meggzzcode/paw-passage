## Top issues (remaining, most important first)
1. **The headline range "35 to 65 pounds" does not match the table.** Where: dek ("six real manager limits run from 35 to 65 pounds"), meta description ("cap dogs at 35 to 65 lb") and the pin text. What: the table lists Cabin Rentals of Georgia as "Under 35 lb" (so a dog of 35 lb is already over) and Hearthside as "35 lb standard; some cabins take larger"; the lowest cap is therefore just under 35. A reader who has a 35-pound dog is told one thing in the dek and another in the table. Costs Trust (4). Fix: edit 1.
2. **Card text still says the same thing twice.** Where: all three pick cards (description line, then "Why we picked it"). What: spec repetition on a card already showing the specs as chips; the "why" should be the job. Fix: edit 2.
3. **Gear remains thin on the actual stay and the buying section is the shortest decision in the page.** Where: "Gear for a big dog, after the rental says yes". What: two of three picks are car gear (hammock, ramp) on a page about renting; the page admits the bed does not fix a slick floor. This is honest, so it is a limit rather than a defect (Buying advice 4). Fix: edit 3 (copy) and optionally 4.
4. **Filler and a weak-ending sentence in the first paragraph.** Where: "If a listing's wording feels vague, ask the host to spell it out." What: filler after a strong paragraph; the Lab story and the 82-pound example both appear before the table. Fix: edit 5.
5. **Pick-card button contrast** (dark green on rust, about 1.8:1 by my estimate). Template; fix: edit 6.

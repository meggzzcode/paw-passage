## What I recorded

### Flippa (seller side)

Sources: https://flippa.com/pricing, https://support.flippa.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000774276 and https://flippa.com/valuation. Neither the pricing page nor the support article shows a date, so I cannot confirm the figures are current.

Listing fee: the pricing page describes four one-time listing packages (a 3-month standard listing and three 6-month packages with more reach) but shows no dollar prices on the page as I could read it. The Flippa valuation page states: The Entry Level Package starts at $29 and success fees start from 3%. The support article says each listing fee covers one asset only and that fees depend on asset type and asking or reserve price.

Success fee: mandatory, paid by the seller, depends on final sale value and whether a broker helps. The exact percentage for a sale of about $1,500 is UNVERIFIED because the tiers are not printed on the pages I could read. Third-party sites (not Flippa) report 3% to 10% or more. I did not rely on them.

Escrow on Flippa (support article, wording as given): Escrow.com fees are paid by the buyer, with a 20% discount on variable fees offered to buyers. A flat escrow fee paid by the seller is usually $20 and may apply to sellers with bank accounts outside the United States. FlippaPay, for sales of US$10,000 and above, starts at 0.5% of the final price and is paid by the buyer.

Add-ons shown on the pricing page: NDA and Confidentiality $199 and Legal Templates $199 on some packages, free on others.

### Escrow.com (official fee page https://www.escrow.com/fee-calculator, US dollars)

Standard fee for $0 to $5,000.00: 2.6%, minimum $50.00. Concierge for the same band: 5.2%, minimum $100.00.
$5,000.01 to $50,000.00: Standard 2.4%, minimum $130.00. Concierge 4.8%.
$50,000.01 to $200,000.00: Standard 1.9%, minimum $1,200.00. Concierge 3.8%.
Payment processing for card or PayPal in the lowest band: Standard or Concierge fee plus 3.05%. Not available in higher bands.
Disbursement to the seller: wire in the U.S. $10.00 (1 business day), wire outside the U.S. $20.00 (3 to 5 business days), electronic check (ACH) U.S. only, no charge (3 business days). A $25.00 intermediary fee is added for an international wire from a buyer outside the U.S.

So a $1,500 sale costs the minimum $50.00 Standard escrow fee (2.6% would be $39). The Flippa support article lists an older looking table (3.25% with a $10 minimum). The two disagree. The Escrow.com page is the official source, so I use it, but the current Flippa checkout may show something different. UNVERIFIED which applies on a Flippa deal.

### Money Nomad (https://moneynomad.com/)

Seller fee: a one-time $297 listing fee. The page says No commissions and that sellers keep 100% of the sale price. No other charge is listed.
Process as described: create a listing with metrics, screenshots and an asking price and pay the fee; the team reviews it for completeness and fit but says it does not audit the numbers; once approved it is marketed to Empire Flippers' buyer list of over 300,000; buyers message the seller and the seller negotiates directly.
Escrow and payment handling: not mentioned on the home page. UNVERIFIED. Terms of Service, FAQ and seller guide pages were not read.
No minimum site age or revenue is stated on the home page.

### Rough cost on a $1,500 sale (arithmetic only, my estimate)

Flippa: $29 listing + success fee (3% would be $45, actual UNVERIFIED) + escrow $50 if the Escrow.com schedule applies, so roughly $124 or more, about 8% of the price.
Money Nomad: $297 listing, no commission, plus whatever escrow the deal uses (about $50 at Escrow.com), so roughly $347, about 23% of the price. The flat fee makes it a poor fit for a $1,500 ask, and it is not refunded if nothing sells (refund terms UNVERIFIED).

# Independent reader review: dog-life-jacket-for-lakes-and-beaches

Method: Playwright Chromium at 390 and 1280 px against http://localhost:4179; first-screen and full-page screenshots viewed (12,406 px tall at 390, 8,751 px at 1280); text and links extracted; external links fetched; FAQ and Sources opened. No prior reviews read. No user testing; these are my observations.

## Roles

1. First-time search visitor: first screen gives the decision rule at once: subtitle "Girth, not weight, sets the size" and intro "A life jacket is worth it if your dog will be on a boat or dock, in deep or open water, or tires quickly. If your dog only wades, skip it." Strong, direct answer. At 390 the first screen is title, subtitle, byline and photo; the disclosure and intro begin right at the fold.
2. Mobile traveler: scanning works (clear H2s, a compact comparison table, a closing checklist), but the page is 12,406 px tall and the three-jacket section runs about 3,000 px (y=3877 to 6942). The table fits at 390 without horizontal scroll (table width 349 in a 350 px container); cells wrap into 3 to 5 lines but stay readable.
3. Skeptical reader: this is the most candid of the three. The page says plainly "Every cell above is the maker's own claim", "its maker claims up to 50% more flotation with no baseline, so read that as marketing", and that the U.S. Coast Guard says there is no standard. That builds trust. It is then undercut by a thin basis for the picks (one 2021 review "used two test dogs, did not measure buoyancy") and by recurring "no source we read" hedging.

## Observable problems

Repeated answers
- Float Coat sizing is stated four times: table ("XXS 13-17 in to XL 36-42 in"), card chip ("XXS to XL, girth 13-42 in"), card text ("The maker sizes it by girth from XXS (13-17 in) to XL (36-42 in)"), and the prose before it ("The Float Coat is the only one of the three with a size for a 13-17 in girth"); then FAQ ("Ruffwear's Float Coat starts at XXS (13-17 in) and EzyDog's DFD runs to XL (90+ lb, 30-48 in girth)").
- DFD figures: table ("XS 19-32 in to XL 30-48 in"), prose ("The DFD has the widest range"), card chip, card text ("XL is 30-48 in, 90+ lb"), FAQ.
- Outward Hound "flotation aid for a conscious dog in calm water... never to leave the dog unattended" appears in the Dawson prose, the card description, the card "Why we picked it", the "What a life jacket does not replace" section ("Outward Hound says not to leave a dog unattended in its jacket"), and two FAQs ("Do dogs need life jackets?", "Does a life jacket mean I can leave my dog alone near water?").
- "Between sizes the makers disagree" is in the fit section and again in the FAQ.
- "Maker says" / "Independent" labels precede the same statements that the FAQ later restates.

Padding / off-topic
- The beach-rules paragraph (Newman-Dailey quote "Dogs are not permitted on the beach or in the pool area...", Twiddy's Kill Devil Hills and Southern Shores hours) and FAQ "Can my dog go on the beach at a rental?" are regional rental-policy material, only loosely about life jackets; a Florida property manager and Outer Banks towns are an arbitrary sample.
- 16 sources, 2,363 words, 7 min read: long for a "when, size and fit" guide. Salt water, algae, swallowed water and cold water each get their own paragraph; useful, but the guide drifts from jackets into general water safety.

Voice
- The "Independent." / "Maker says." / "Our read." / "Our suggestion." lead-ins are mechanical and are explained in the intro ("Below, Maker says marks manufacturer details and Independent marks guidance from vets, kennel clubs and public agencies"), which is meta text a reader should not need.
- Hedging and defensive lines repeat: "No source we read sets a rule, so this is our judgment", "we read no state boating page, so check the posted rules", "We can't assess a dog from here", "these checks come from two sources, not a standard".
- Natural, concrete lines work well: "Skip it for a dog that only wades at the edge." and "Walk away from it if the jacket slides to one side, rubs under the front legs, or your dog struggles to paddle."

Tables
- One table. Appropriate (three items, same attributes), readable at 390. Issue: ambiguous first row "XS 19-32 in to XL 30-48 in" overlaps itself (XS up to 32 in while XL starts at 30), so it can read as XS being 19-32 in. The other rows use a different pattern, so the girth column is not directly comparable. The Outward Hound row has no girth data at all.
- The table is then restated by three cards, so the information appears twice.

Product cards
- Three cards, each tied to a use ("FOR A BIG DOG", "FOR THE SMALLEST DOGS", "FOR CALM WATER, WITHIN REACH"), introduced by a prose reason. That is good. They do interrupt flow somewhat: each sits mid-section and then a new paragraph begins, giving three 400 px blocks plus a table in sequence.
- "Why we picked it" mostly repeats the sizing chips ("The maker lists sizes XS to XL by girth... a padded back grab handle and a leash D-ring"). The DFD pick rests on one 2021 review of two test dogs; the page says so, but still labels it "top paws".
- Chip "XS 15-25 lbs to XL 90+ lbs" on the DFD card contradicts the page's own "ignore weight" instruction.

Sources / citations
- Inline publisher links are used well (AKC, VCA, Cornell, CDC, National Geographic). Sources list of 16 is collapsed and dated. Some references are dated (Whole Dog Journal 2016 and 2021, 2013 Coast Guard video); the page flags the dates in text.

Disclosure
- One top notice, Amazon sentence, no per-card labels. Clear. "I earn" vs "we" mismatch.

Links
- All internal links 200. External all 200 except cdc.gov (403, bot block; not a confirmed dead link). Amazon links tagged.

Safety / facts
- Responsible: supervise, calm-water flotation aid only, algae, salt water, ask the vet. No errors spotted. Possible gap: the Whole Dog Journal "top paws" pick for a jacket whose maker gives no flotation rating deserves the cautious wording it gets. The closing checklist items are plain list items (no checkboxes), unlike the other two guides.

## Verdict: READY WITH MINOR FIXES

1. Remove repeats: pick one place for each sizing figure (table or card, not both plus prose plus FAQ) and say the Outward Hound "calm water, supervised" point once or twice, not five times.
2. Clarify the DFD table row (give XS and XL ranges explicitly) and drop the weight chip on the DFD card.
3. Trim or cut the beach-rules paragraph and its FAQ; trim the "no source we read" hedges and the "Maker says/Independent" explanatory sentence in the intro.
4. Make "Why we picked it" give a reason beyond restating specs.
5. Fix disclosure voice ("I" to "we").

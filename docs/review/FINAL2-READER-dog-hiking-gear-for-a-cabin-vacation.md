# FINAL2 reader review: dog-hiking-gear-for-a-cabin-vacation

Viewed at 390 and 1280 px (first screen and full page). Role: casual hiker, mobile and skeptical.

## What works
- First screen gives the answer: "Most walks need a leash, water and waste bags." Intro repeats the "four things" with a clear "everything else depends on the trail".
- Checklist ("Trail-day checklist") sits directly under the four-item list, about one screen below the intro. Easy to find. The "On this page" box is collapsed on mobile, so it is not a help.
- Only three product cards, each with a "skip it if" condition ("skip it if you already carry a bowl", "Skip each one if your hike does not have its problem"). A casual hiker is not pushed to buy five things.
- No horizontal scroll at 390; internal links returned 200.

## Observable defects
1. Repetition: the "leash, water, bowl, waste bags" list appears in the dek, the intro ("Most cabin hikes need four things"), the "What to bring" list and the checklist. Four passes of one answer in the first ~1.5 screens.
2. The lede says "Most walks need a leash, water and waste bags" (three); the intro says "four things" (adds a bowl). Small inconsistency.
3. The Quencher bowl card sits first among the "gear for which problem" cards although the page says "Any collapsible one works" under "A bowl". A card for a thing the page calls generic; its own reason is only "saves carrying a rigid one".
4. Light and leash recommendations (Beacon, Knot-a-Leash) are named in prose with no card or link, so a reader who wants them has to leave the page; inconsistent with "All three cards below".
5. The checklist item "Steep ground: harness fitted at the cabin" mixes a to-do with a conditional, so it cannot be ticked cleanly; the checklist also has no print or share affordance next to it (Print/Copy link only at the page foot).
6. Boots paragraph is a dense wall (sizing, practice, 15-minute check, NPS, AKC, Outward Hound, Orvis) in one paragraph on mobile.
7. "What we could not confirm" admits no source gives a boot cutoff; fine and honest, but the boots card still appears for the casual reader whose default answer is "probably not needed".

## 1. Rules (read first, apply to every step)

- Work only in the owner's already-signed-in Chrome sessions. **Never type or read passwords, codes or recovery data.** Stop at any CAPTCHA, bot wall, security prompt, upgrade or payment prompt, and say which page stopped you; never work around it.
- **No purchases, no Add to Cart / Add to List, no signing in to any retailer, no changes to any affiliate account (Amazon Associates, Impact) or to the repo.** Do not open the SiteStripe or affiliate tools; you are reading public product pages only.
- **Do not copy review text** and do not record star ratings or review counts. **Do not download Amazon photos** (the site shows no product photos). Screenshots stay in the private folder; the table records file names only.
- **No prices.** If you must note something about cost, use a band (under $25, $25 to $50, $50 to $100, over $100) in the private notes, not in the returned table.
- **UNVERIFIED beats guessing.** If a value is not on the page you are looking at, write `UNVERIFIED`. Never infer an ASIN, a size, a certification or a spec from a similarly named product. Do not fabricate an ASIN: take it only from the Amazon URL (`/dp/XXXXXXXXXX`) or the "Product information" section of the page you actually opened.
- Availability: record "currently available to purchase" as dated evidence for this brief only (for example "Add to Cart present on 2026-11-26"). The site does not display stock; this is not copy.
- Amazon pages differ by browser location and by variant selector: record the exact variant selected when you read (size, color, count) and whether the ASIN in the address bar changes when you change the selector.
- The maker's own page is preferred evidence. Paraphrase facts in your own words, three to six bullets; do not paste marketing sentences.
- Order of work: **Part 0 (CPS), then Batches 1 to 6 in order.** Stop a batch early if you hit a bot wall; report what is done.

## 2. What to record for EVERY candidate

1. Exact title shown on the U.S. Amazon product page (amazon.com), brand, and model name or number.
2. **ASIN** (from the URL or Product information), and the selected variant, size and package count.
3. Whether it is currently available to purchase (Add to Cart or Buy Now present; seller shown; "Sold by"/"Ships from"), with the date and time.
4. Key dimensions or capacities and the intended use as the page states them.
5. Any conflicting specs (Amazon vs maker page; two statements on one page).
6. Manufacturer documentation link (product page, manual, size chart or certification record), and whether it describes the same model and variant as the Amazon page.
7. Whether the Amazon item is the same model and variant that our evidence supports (answer YES / NO / UNCLEAR, with the reason).
8. Reasons NOT to recommend it (conflicts, missing detail, third-party seller on a safety item, an old model, bundles, used or resale condition).
9. Whether it already exists in the Gear catalog (the id is in the tables below; do not check the live site).
10. An alternative if unsupported (a different catalog id or candidate id from this brief).
11. Research date.
12. Category extras (rows below, and the "Specific checks" column).

## 5. Category extras (summary; the specific checks are in each batch)

- **Restraints and crates:** the CPS record must name the exact model and size; the Amazon title, ASIN and size must match; the strap kit is a separate item to verify; prefer the maker link for a model Amazon sells in a different variant.
- **Boots and coats:** size and fit chart, materials, conditions.
- **Paw wax:** ingredients, intended use, cautions.
- **Enrichment:** sizes, filling, cleaning, supervision warnings, maker instructions.
- **RV accessories:** power needs, operating conditions, limits; no safety claim for unattended pets.
- **Gifts:** how easy the item is to choose without measurements.

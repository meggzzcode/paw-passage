# Path to 9s (proposal, nothing executed yet)

## 1. What is and is not in our control

Of the 10 scoring criteria, eight are decided by what we write and build, so a 9 is reachable by editing, merging and deleting. Two are not fully ours:

- **Organic traffic opportunity.** Today's 2-6 reflects a brand-new site on competitive queries. We can raise it by only keeping pages on queries where the results looked weak or host-side, but a true 9 needs ranking proof (90 days of Search Console data). Realistic before proof: 7-8.
- **Monetization.** Needs approved programs and real link placements. Realistic before approvals: 6-7; reachable 8-9 only for gear pages and a live stays section once approvals exist.

So the honest target is: 9 on the eight controllable criteria for every page we keep, 7-8 on the two external ones now, rising to 9 when traffic and approvals arrive. Anything that cannot reach that gets merged or deleted.

## 2. What a 9 means (checkable, not vibes)

| Criterion | A page scores 9 only if |
|---|---|
| SEO targeting | One primary query, plain-language title, no cannibalization with another page, title and H1 match what the page delivers |
| Intent alignment | The first screen gives the answer the headline promises |
| Content quality | Every factual claim sourced or labelled as our judgment; no unverifiable first-hand claims; fact-checked by someone who did not write it |
| Originality | Uses our 38-manager data, a tool, or an original comparison nobody else publishes |
| Audience appeal | Opens on a real owner problem; one clear next step; reads as a friend who did the homework |
| Natural writing | No repeated hedge phrases (cap: 2 per page), no templated section openers, one plain "how sure we are" box, 1,200-2,000 words unless a table-heavy reference |
| Shareability | A downloadable, printable or graphic asset on the page |
| Maintenance/resale | Every dated number has a "last checked" date and an entry in a re-check log; no unneeded dated claims |

An independent reviewer re-scores each page against this table. Rule: a page that cannot reach 8.5 on the eight controllable criteria after rework is deleted or merged, not kept.

## 4. Sequence

1. **Decide** (this approval): the table above.
2. **Week 1: clean-up.** Compliance fixes; delete and merge with redirects; typo, citation and unverifiable-claim fixes; link all four tools from the homepage, footer and guides; state the 38-manager proof on the homepage; refresh BUYER_HANDOVER.
3. **Weeks 1-3: data layer.** Reconcile CSV vs TS counts; add pool/hot-tub, minimum-age and unattended-pet fields; publish the dataset page with a CSV download; create the re-check log.
4. **Weeks 2-5: rewrite the 15 core guides** to the table in section 2, one house voice guide first (plain, warm, one "how sure we are" box, no templated openers), each with a downloadable asset.
5. **Weeks 3-12: new data-led pieces**, only the top picks (no-pet-fee, true cost, fee data page, reactive dog, Christmas cabin, romantic weekend), each built to the same standard.
6. **Gate:** an independent re-audit with the section 2 rubric after each batch. Pages under 8.5 on the controllable criteria are fixed once more, then deleted.
7. **After 90 days:** read Search Console and GA4 to turn the two external criteria from estimates into evidence.

## 5. What this will not do

It cannot make traffic or affiliate income exist; it makes the site as strong as it can be before they do. It also relies on the independent reviewer being tougher than the writers, which is why the rubric is checkable.

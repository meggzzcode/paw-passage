## What works well
- The three-clocks content: 24 hours to respond, 14 days to claim, 60 days to appeal, each sourced page by page, with the Hometime 72-hour and 30-day figures flagged as conflicting with Airbnb's pages. Nothing in the top results puts these together.
- The "What is not published" table and "How sure we are" block state exactly what no source says and give the reader a question to ask for each gap, which is rare and trust-building.
- The three reply scripts (ask for detail, pay part, decline) plus an honesty line ("Send the decline only if it is true") and a pre-booking message to send the host; they are directly usable.

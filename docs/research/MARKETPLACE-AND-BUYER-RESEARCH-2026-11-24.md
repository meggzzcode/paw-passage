## 6. Recommendation

1. List first on Flippa Basic ($29), asking $1,500 with offers invited, listing text above. Pay the $29 only after checking the live pricing page and confirming starter sites are accepted. Basic's 30-day term matches the plan; relist or upgrade only if the triggers below fire.
2. List second on SideProjectors (free) the same day, emphasizing the tools, dataset and codebase.
3. Direct outreach, 5 to 10 individual messages in week 1 to pet-travel and dog-site owners found above, plus any Feedspot-listed blogs; one message each, no mass mailing, escrow only. Post on forums only after reading their rules, and avoid off-platform payment.

Price triggers (my estimate; the earlier reports put realistic closing at $600 to $1,200):

| Day | Condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | 0 enquiries | Check listing quality and title; keep $1,500 and send outreach |
| 8 | 1 to 3 enquiries, no offer | Hold price |
| 14 | 0 enquiries, or fewer than 3 and no offer | Cut ask to $1,200 |
| 14 | Any written offer at or above $900 | Counter at $1,200 to $1,300 |
| 21 | No offer above $600 | Cut ask to $900 and consider Startup tier only if there were 5 or more enquiries |
| 21 | Offer of $700 or more via escrow | Accept the best offer; a 30-day sale beats an unsold listing |
| 30 | No offers | List domain and content separately or relist at $600 |

Caveats: no verified pre-revenue sold data; Flippa official fees and rules unread; the outreach list is leads, not confirmed buyers; not legal or tax advice.

## Top issues (most important first)
1. Where: spec card headings for the iCrate, Pawtect Pads and Petmate play yard. What: brand repeated on the card (eyebrow plus name), which looks careless on three of four cards. Fix: strip a leading brand from the product display name when it equals the card's brand line (template), or rename the records to "iCrate Dog Crate", "Pawtect Pads (washable pee pads, 2 pack)" and "Precision Pet Soft Side Play Yard".
2. Where: "What to buy first", item 1 ("The MidWest iCrate with its divider, if your puppy already sleeps in a crate... If not, start with the pads."). What: the ordering is right but two cases are squeezed into one numbered item, so a skimmer reads "crate first, pads second" even if the puppy is not crate-trained. Fix: open the list with "Two cases: a puppy that already sleeps in a crate starts with the iCrate; one that is not house-trained starts with the Pawtect Pads. Then, for either:" and number the iCrate and pads as 1a and 1b, or put the pads first and the crate second with "if your puppy already sleeps in a crate".
3. Where: pad paragraph, "The pick card below links to the smallest pack, Extra Small (18 x 24 in)". What: the main pad button leads to the smallest size while the chip says "XS 18x24 to L 41x41 in"; a buyer who skims may order a pack too small for a sleeping spot. Fix: add to the card's why line "Choose the size on the listing" (already said in prose) or point the link at the product page without a size preselected.
4. Where: "It suits a puppy that already sleeps in a crate at home" (end of the iCrate paragraph). What: it repeats the opening of the paragraph ("If your puppy already sleeps in a crate at home, bring one") and the "What to buy first" item. Fix: delete that sentence.
5. Where: "and some reviewers on it report latch and edge complaints". What: reviewer opinion with no count and no way to check, in a page that otherwise sources every claim; at odds with the "no ratings" stance. Fix: replace with "the page gives no wire gauge, weights or folded dimensions, so test the latch before the trip."

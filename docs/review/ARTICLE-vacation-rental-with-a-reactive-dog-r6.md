## Issues
1. Where: dek vs "A rental cannot change how your dog reacts...". What: opening restates the dek. Fix: keep the dek, replace paragraph 1 with the concrete detail only: "The listing photo shows a cozy cabin, not the driveway shared by four other cabins that decides whether the first morning is a walk or a scene. Check that before you book."
2. Where: whole page body. What: inline publisher links are almost absent (only Airbnb once, Humane Colorado once); most claims name "ASPCA", "Cornell", "Fear Free", "Best Friends" in plain text and rely on 9 px superscripts. Style guide section 7 wants the publisher as an inline link. Fix: link the first mention of each publisher per section, keep the marker only where no publisher is named.
3. Where: "What to buy" (crate, gate, KONG). What: the page itself says none blocks the view or changes the behavior, and the gate "will not stop a dog that can jump 26 in". A reactive-dog owner would want a visual barrier or white noise, which is not offered. Honest, but the picks are weak. Fix: cut to the crate and KONG, or add one pick that matches "block the view" (window film or a crate cover) if a verified product exists.
4. Where: "Watch for stress." bullet. What: the ID-tag advice ("Check the ID too: Fear Free recommends a microchip...") is jammed into a stress-signs bullet. Fix: move it to "Doors, entry and exit routes" or drop it.
5. Where: first-day plan, "Cover the windows ... 6" and marker boxes in general. What: markers are 9 x 15 px, small for a thumb. Fix: add padding or an invisible ::after hit area of at least 24 px.
6. Where: "The short answer" bullet 1. What: "Check four things... what is next door, any shared wall, entrance or path, what your dog can see..." has a comma list that reads as three or five items. Fix: use a numbered list or semicolons.

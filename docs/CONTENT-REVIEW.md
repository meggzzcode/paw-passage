# Content review: free adventure library (schema v2)

Review date: 2026-11-26. This document is public: it describes only the free adventures and the aggregate method. Reviews of premium material are kept privately and are not recorded here.

**Honest statement about the scores.** The 1 to 10 numbers in each adventure file are the independent reviewer's scores, not the authors' own (the authors scored about 0.8 points higher on average and were judged too generous). Publication bar (chosen by the owner on 2026-11-26): average of at least 7.0 and no criterion below 6, with an editorial goal of 7.5 or more for premium adventures. Nothing has been tested with dogs or owners. The scores are editorial judgements, not customer ratings, and must never be shown or described as such. The bar is an authoring gate, not a quality guarantee.

**Status.** The Little Picnic Club and The Sunrise Breakfast Club did not pass the bar after a rewrite and were deleted. The free library is the 10 published adventures.

## Research actually read

Fetched and read in full:

1. Cornell Riney Canine Health Center, "canine enrichment ideas" (riney.vet.cornell.edu). Informed: sniff-led walks where the dog chooses the pace, hidden-food and scent games, learning through positive reinforcement, supervise toys and DIY items.
2. Cornell Riney Canine Health Center, "senior dog enrichment" (vet.cornell.edu). Informed: seniors differ, keep outings short and easy and sniff-focused, scent games and simple tricks indoors, talk to your vet about safe activities.
3. VCA Animal Hospitals, enrichment and foraging toys (vcahospitals.com). Informed: start easy, durable size-appropriate items, supervise, watch for food guarding, check ingredients with your vet.
4. ASPCA, canine DIY enrichment (aspca.org). Informed: household scent games, DIY items, supervise and remove anything a dog tries to swallow.
5. Dogs Trust, enrichment activities for dogs (dogstrust.org.uk). Informed: non-toxic right-sized items, count enrichment food in daily portions, fresh water, check what is safe for your dog.

Could **not** be read, so **not cited**: the AAHA enrichment pages (the fetch returned 403; only search-result snippets were seen). The earlier guessed ASPCA and Dogs Trust URLs returned 404; the working pages above were found via search and then fetched. Nothing was copied from any source or from any dog-adventure book. Ideas were paraphrased into original designs. Each adventure lists only the sources that actually informed it; none of the sources describes the adventures themselves.

## Changes made

- Every adventure is schema v2 (reveal, objective, completion, activeMinutes, supplies, optional supplies, household substitutes, controlled `requires`, transport, homes, space, places, avoidWeather, personalities hint, dog and owner mobility, step-free, crowd, strangers, other dogs, noise, food, water, safety, activity types, keepsake family and theme, research, review).
- The scent-trail and blanket-fort adventures from the first draft were merged into **The Secret Detective Agency** and **The Living Room Campout** (new ids; the old ids were pre-release and removed).
- Reconciled with the owner's brief: durations and budgets follow its ranges where they suit the design; keepsake names follow it (Official Toy Champion, Founding Member, Safari Explorer, Captain's Route, Chief Taste Tester, Campground Pass, Greetings from Our Town). Deliberate deviations: the Sunrise club keeps a seasonal frame (there is no sunrise postcard artwork) and the Campout keeps its ticket artwork; the Taste-Off (formerly titled as a bake-off, retitled because nothing is baked) is a no-recipe tasting contest of foods the dog already eats, because that is the safer design, and it gained a "bakery counter" step.
- Sunrise Breakfast Club first scored 7.67 and was redesigned with a nose-driven "morning briefing"; it now averages 8.0. Memory Lane first scored below 7 on dog enjoyment (the dog was a bystander); the dog now sniffs an item from the old photo and revisits the spot.
- Tourists in Our Own Town now allows choosing a less familiar corner of town.
- Collections replaced with the 20 owner-defined collections. 14 have at least one free adventure; 6 have none and are marked premium-only with honest copy that names no premium adventure.

## Removed: The Slow-Lane Sniffari

An extra adventure from our first draft. We removed it because its core (a slow, accessible, sniff-led outing where the dog decides the pace) overlaps other planned gentle-outing material and, within the free set, overlaps The Pick-a-Path Expedition (dog leads the route) and the Safari (dog's nose and ears guide the walk). Its originality was already our weakest score (7). Senior and limited-mobility dogs remain well served by the Handshake Club, Sunrise Breakfast Club, Memory Lane, Paw-trait Studio and indoor adventures.

## Second pass: redundancy and variety

- Mechanics are spread across: bracket and choice (Showdown), trail-and-find (Detective), blanket scene (Picnic, Campout), photography (Paw-trait, Memory Lane), checklist walk (Safari, Tourists), choice at forks (Pick-a-Path), observe-and-notice (Sunrise, Handshake) and tasting contest (Taste-Off).
- Postcards appear three times (Picnic, Tourists, Pick-a-Path); each has a different core mechanic and keepsake theme.
- Campout and Sunrise are both calm companionship adventures; they differ by time of day, setup and artwork.
- Coverage: food-free (11 of 12), no-car (all 12), apartment (all 12), low-prep (11 of 12 need no more than light prep; 8 cost nothing), seated or step-free owner (5), senior (all 12), puppy (9 of 12), quiet or nervous dogs (the indoor and home-spot adventures).
- A senior apartment dog can earn 8 different stamps from the free set. The Beach Explorer stamp cannot be earned from the free set, and its rule says so.

## Remaining concerns

- Several adventures sit between 7.2 and 7.4 and could drop under the bar with a harsher reviewer; originality (6 on six of the twelve) and adventure factor are the weakest criteria library-wide.
- The rewritten Paw-trait Studio, Little Picnic Club and Sunrise Breakfast Club need an independent re-score before they can be published again.
- No free adventure needs a car, water or a beach, so those dog profiles are served only by the premium library.
- The Taste-Off is the only food adventure. It is deliberately vet-deferential, with a no-food Sniff-Off variation. The site states plainly that nothing is vet-reviewed.
- Several outdoor adventures declare crowd "some" with strangers and other dogs, so they are correctly held back from nervous dogs; the indoor and home-spot set carries those profiles.
- Content has not been tested with real dogs or owners, and the scores are editorial judgements (see the top of this document).

## Survey match gap pass on the free set (2026-11-26)

Each free user now takes a short dog survey and gets one match from the free pool, so every dog type needs at least one safe, strong free match. Audited the 10 published free adventures against 16 dog and home types. The first-pass audit found no free match for: lively or hyper dogs (none declared energy "lively"), two-dog households and families with kids (no `household` tags), puppy-friendly outdoor or surface play, and shy or reactive dogs who want something with a little movement (every quiet card was gentle, and every outdoor card meets strangers and other dogs).

Eight new free adventures were drafted, each with a different mechanic from the existing set and from each other. An independent scorer then reviewed all eight (verdicts in the premium-content gap3 folder). Outcome: 2 kept, 2 pending a re-score, 4 deleted. Free keepsake themes remain unique across the free cards.

- **Kept (2), fixed, independent scores applied to the `review` block:** The Dog's Time Capsule Dig (adv-d9g5p2; 7, 7, 6, 6, 8, 8, mean 7.0) now seals the paper note in a zip bag, out of reach of digging. The Photo Finish Lane (adv-z7c4v5; 7, 8, 6, 7, 7, 7, mean 7.0) now lists puppy, adult and senior to match its variations and notes a bowl of fresh water near the lane (the `water` field stays `none`, since that field describes water activities).
- **Re-scored:** The Low-Down Tour (adv-l4d7h3) passed the independent re-score at 7.33 after its group was capped at 2 or 3 people staying 6 or more feet back and its payoff was strengthened; it is back in the repo as published. The Twin Mailroom (adv-t6w2n9) scored 6.83 and was deleted.
- **Deleted (4):** The Cardboard Canyon Run (adv-b3r8x4, generic and overlapping), The Texture Trail (adv-x2f6k8), The Sundial Dog (adv-u5s9a7) and The Bedtime Story Audition (adv-y8e3j6), all scored below the bar by the independent scorer.

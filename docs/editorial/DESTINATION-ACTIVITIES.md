## Activities and sources

### georgia/blue-ridge

- **Aska Adventure Area** (trail). Dogs stay on a leash here, per the visitor site. Source: Blue Ridge Mountains visitor site, https://www.blueridgemountains.com/blog/fido-friendly-blue-ridge/. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Lake Blue Ridge, Morganton Point** (water). The page states no rule for this site. The Forest Service says dogs in campgrounds must be on a leash and under control, and to use a leash in crowded areas. Read the posted signs on arrival. Source: Blue Ridge Mountains visitor site, https://www.blueridgemountains.com/blog/fido-friendly-blue-ridge/; also U.S. Forest Service, https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/chattahoochee-oconee/safety-ethics/enjoy-outdoors. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Toccoa River Park, McCaysville** (park). The fenced dog area is leash-free, per the visitor site. The page gives no hours. Source: Blue Ridge Mountains visitor site, https://www.blueridgemountains.com/blog/fido-friendly-blue-ridge/. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Blue Ridge Adventure Dog** (park). Dogs are welcome in the outdoor park, per the visitor site. The page does not list entry rules or fees. Source: Blue Ridge Mountains visitor site, https://www.blueridgemountains.com/blog/fido-friendly-blue-ridge/. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Blue Ridge Private Dog Park** (park). Reserved by the hour, so the space is yours alone, per the visitor site. Source: Blue Ridge Mountains visitor site, https://www.blueridgemountains.com/blog/fido-friendly-blue-ridge/. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Toccoa Riverside Restaurant** (patio). The visitor site lists a "Pooch Patio" and a canine menu. It states no leash rule. Source: Blue Ridge Mountains visitor site, https://www.blueridgemountains.com/blog/fido-friendly-blue-ridge/. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Bear Claw Vineyards** (tasting-room). Explore Georgia says you can relax on the covered patio with your dog. It states no leash rule. Source: Explore Georgia, https://exploregeorgia.org/list/georgias-pet-friendly-wineries. Read 2026-11-24.

### georgia/ellijay

- **Harrison Park and River Park** (park). The chamber calls both pet-friendly. The page states no leash rule, so bring a leash. Source: Gilmer County Chamber, https://www.gilmerchamber.com/blog/explore-the-ellijays/ellijay-georgia-a-pet-friendly-paradise/. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Bear Creek Trail** (trail). The page names it as pet-friendly and states no leash rule, so bring a leash. Source: Gilmer County Chamber, https://www.gilmerchamber.com/blog/explore-the-ellijays/ellijay-georgia-a-pet-friendly-paradise/. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Roo Mountain Vineyards** (tasting-room). Explore Georgia says to bring a leashed, well-behaved dog. Source: Explore Georgia, https://exploregeorgia.org/list/georgias-pet-friendly-wineries. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Ott Vineyards and Winery** (tasting-room). Leashed dogs are welcome in the outdoor areas, per Explore Georgia. Source: Explore Georgia, https://exploregeorgia.org/list/georgias-pet-friendly-wineries. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Engelheim Vineyards** (tasting-room). Dogs are welcome, per Explore Georgia. The page states no leash or indoor rule. Source: Explore Georgia, https://exploregeorgia.org/list/georgias-pet-friendly-wineries. Read 2026-11-24.

### georgia/helen

- **Helen to Unicoi Trail** (trail). Dogs must be kept on a leash, per the Helen visitor site. At Unicoi State Park the leash may be six feet at most. Source: Alpine Helen visitor site, https://helenga.org/helen-to-unicoi-trail/; also Georgia State Parks, https://gastateparks.org/TailsOnTrailsClub. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Helen to Hardman Heritage Trail** (trail). The visitor site calls the trail pet friendly and states no leash rule, so keep a leash on. Source: Alpine Helen visitor site, https://helenga.org/helen-to-hardman-heritage-trail/. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Smithgall Woods State Park** (trail). Georgia State Parks welcome leashed dogs on trails. A ParkPass fee applies to visit. Source: Georgia State Parks, https://gastateparks.org/DogWalking; also Georgia State Parks, https://gastateparks.org/SmithgallWoods. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Anna Ruby Falls** (trail). The visitor site calls the path dog-friendly. The Forest Service page states no pet rule, so keep your dog leashed and under control. Source: Alpine Helen visitor site, https://helenga.org/you-need-a-hike-starter-hikes-near-helen-ga-for-people-who-need-to-get-away/; also U.S. Forest Service, https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/chattahoochee-oconee/recreation/anna-ruby-falls-recreation-area. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Cowboys & Angels** (patio). The visitor site lists a "Pet Friendly Deck". It states no leash or indoor rule. Source: Alpine Helen visitor site, https://helenga.org/cowboys-angels-restaurant-bar/. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Habersham Vineyards & Winery** (tasting-room). Leashed dogs are allowed outside and in the pavilion, per Explore Georgia. Source: Explore Georgia, https://exploregeorgia.org/list/georgias-pet-friendly-wineries. Read 2026-11-24.

Source: Dahlonega-Lumpkin County Visitors Bureau, https://www.dahlonega.org/articles/post/exploring-dahlonega-with-your-pet-must-visit-spots-for-you-and-your-furry-friend. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Etowah River Trail** (trail). The visitors bureau asks that you always keep your dog on a leash (its general rule, not a posted trail rule). Source: Dahlonega-Lumpkin County Visitors Bureau, https://www.dahlonega.org/articles/post/exploring-dahlonega-with-your-pet-must-visit-spots-for-you-and-your-furry-friend. Read 2026-11-24.

- **Wolf Mountain Vineyards & Winery** (tasting-room). Leashed dogs are welcome on the lower-level patios, per Explore Georgia. Source: Explore Georgia, https://exploregeorgia.org/list/georgias-pet-friendly-wineries. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Montaluce Winery & Restaurant** (tasting-room). Explore Georgia says you can sit with your dog at the outside tables. It states no leash rule. Source: Explore Georgia, https://exploregeorgia.org/list/georgias-pet-friendly-wineries. Read 2026-11-24.

### california/san-diego

- **Dog Beach, Ocean Beach** (beach). Leash-free, listed as open 24 hours. Owners use it at their own risk and are responsible for their dogs; outside leash-free areas, dogs must be leashed in most city parks. Source: City of San Diego, https://sandiego.gov/park-and-recreation/parks/dogs/leashfree; also City of San Diego, https://sandiego.gov/park-and-recreation/parks/dogs/bchdog. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Fiesta Island, Mission Bay Park** (park). Leash-optional, shoreline included. The entry now says the city's pages give conflicting opening hours and gives none. Two tern nesting sites are gated year-round, the buffer zones around them are closed April 15 through September 15, and dogs are barred from the Youth Aquatic Center. Source: City of San Diego, https://sandiego.gov/park-and-recreation/parks/dogs/bchdog; also City of San Diego, https://sandiego.gov/park-and-recreation/parks/dogs/leashfree. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Other city beaches** (beach). Dogs are not allowed on the beach, boardwalk or adjacent parks from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. between Nov. 1 and Mar. 31, or 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. between Apr. 1 and Oct. 31. At other hours they must be on a leash. Source: City of San Diego Lifeguards, https://www.sandiego.gov/lifeguards/safety/bchreg. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Balboa Park, Nate's Point and Morley Field** (park). Both are listed as off-leash, open 24 hours. Elsewhere in city parks, trails and canyons, dogs must be on a leash no longer than 8 feet. Source: City of San Diego, https://sandiego.gov/park-and-recreation/parks/dogs/leashfree; also City of San Diego, https://www.sandiego.gov/park-and-recreation/parks/dogs. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Coronado North Beach Dog Run** (beach). Listed as off-leash around the clock. Dogs must be leashed until they reach the beach. Source: San Diego Tourism Authority, https://sandiego.org/articles/pets/California-dog-beaches-parks-recreation-areas-in-san-diego.aspx. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Del Mar dog beaches** (beach). Off-season (day after Labor Day through June 15), dogs may be off leash on North Beach under voice control. In summer (June 16 through Labor Day) dogs must be leashed except from dawn to 8 a.m. on North Beach, and no dogs are allowed on Main Beach in those summer months. The city lists South Beach as leashed all year. Source: City of Del Mar, https://www.delmar.ca.us/FAQ.aspx?QID=96. Read 2026-11-24.

### california/santa-barbara

- **Douglas Family Preserve** (park). Dogs may be off leash in certain areas if they stay under sight and voice control. Carry a leash and use it when needed. Source: City of Santa Barbara, https://sbparksandrec.santabarbaraca.gov/park/douglas-family-preserve. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Shoreline Park** (park). Dogs on leash, per the city's park page. Source: City of Santa Barbara, https://sbparksandrec.santabarbaraca.gov/parks/shoreline-park. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Chase Palm Park** (park). Leash required, per Visit Santa Barbara. Source: Visit Santa Barbara, https://santabarbaraca.com/plan-your-trip/know-before-you-go/pet-friendly-santa-barbara/. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Funk Zone tasting rooms** (tasting-room). Rules vary by room. Kunin, Whitcraft and The Valley Project want dogs on a leash, and Margerum and the Santa Barbara Wine Collective allow dogs on the patio only. Source: Visit Santa Barbara, https://santabarbaraca.com/itinerary/dog-friendly-wineries-in-santa-barbara/. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Downtown patios** (patio). Dogs of all sizes are allowed downtown on a leash, and can join you on open-air dining patios, per Visit Santa Barbara. Source: Visit Santa Barbara, https://santabarbaraca.com/plan-your-trip/know-before-you-go/pet-friendly-santa-barbara/. Read 2026-11-24.

### california/monterey

- **Carmel Beach** (beach). Dogs may be off leash if they stay under voice control. The city's test: within 25 feet of you and back within three feet on command. Leash again at the stairways and elsewhere in town, including the Scenic Pathway. Source: City of Carmel-by-the-Sea, https://ci.carmel.ca.us/post/beach-rules; also City of Carmel-by-the-Sea, https://ci.carmel.ca.us/post/animal-services. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Carmel restaurant patios** (patio). The village visitor site says many restaurants seat dogs on patios, and a few indoors. Policies are set by each restaurant. Source: Carmel-by-the-Sea visitor site, https://www.carmelcalifornia.com/dog_friendly-dog_friendly_restaurants_604.htm. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Carmel River State Beach** (beach). Dogs allowed on leash at all times. Watch for seasonal snowy plover resting areas in late winter and early spring; the page gives no dates. Source: California State Parks, https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=567. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Asilomar State Beach, Pacific Grove** (beach). Dogs on leash are allowed on the beach, trails and conference grounds, not in conference buildings. The park page posts closure alerts; on 2026-11-24 it listed the beach and Coast Trail as closed for large swells and high tides, so check it first. Source: California State Parks, https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=566. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Fort Ord Dunes State Park** (trail). Leashed dogs are allowed on the recreation trail that runs north and south. They are not allowed on the spur trails to the beach or on the beach, to protect bird habitat. Source: California State Parks, https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=580. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Monterey Dog Park, El Estero Park** (park). Open dawn to dusk, with separate areas for small and larger dogs. Elsewhere in the city dogs must be on a leash no longer than six feet. Source: City of Monterey, https://monterey.gov/your_city_hall/departments/parks_and_recreation/parks/dog_park.php. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Monterey State Historic Park** (park). Dogs are allowed but not inside park buildings. The statewide leash limit is six feet. Source: California State Parks, https://www.parks.ca.gov/Dogs. Read 2026-11-24.

### california/mendocino

- **Mendocino Headlands State Park** (trail). The state lists dogs as allowed and names no restriction here. Statewide, the leash is six feet at most. Source: California State Parks, https://www.parks.ca.gov/Dogs; also California State Parks, https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=442. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Russian Gulch State Park** (trail). Dogs allowed in the campground, on the beach and on trails west of Highway 1. Not allowed on Fern Canyon Trail or trails east of Highway 1. Source: California State Parks, https://www.parks.ca.gov/Dogs. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Van Damme State Park** (beach). Dogs allowed in the campground, on the beach and at Spring Ranch. Not allowed on Fern Canyon Trail, Old Logging Road Trail or Pygmy Forest Trail. Source: California State Parks, https://www.parks.ca.gov/Dogs. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Jug Handle State Natural Reserve** (trail). Dogs allowed on the beach and headland trails west of Highway 1. Not allowed on the Staircase Trail or east of Highway 1. Source: California State Parks, https://www.parks.ca.gov/Dogs. Read 2026-11-24.
- **MacKerricher State Park** (beach). Dogs are allowed in the park except at the Seal Rookery, Virgin Creek Beach, and Ten Mile Beach and Dunes. Source: California State Parks, https://www.parks.ca.gov/Dogs. Read 2026-11-24.
- **Noyo Headlands Trail, Fort Bragg** (trail). Dogs on leash are welcome, per the Mendocino Land Trust. Source: Mendocino Land Trust, https://www.mendocinolandtrust.org/trails/noyo-headlands-trail/. Read 2026-11-24.

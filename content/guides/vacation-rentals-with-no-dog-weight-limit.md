## Are there vacation rentals with no dog weight limit?

Yes, a few. Of the 38 vacation rental managers whose public pet pages we read (a convenience sample, not a survey, read 2026-11-18), two, Sliding Rock Cabins and Hapey Cabin Rentals, say outright they have no weight limit, six state a number, and 30 say nothing about weight on the page we read. That silence is not a promise. A listing that says 50 pounds does not always end the search for a 70-pound dog.

- Stated limits run from 35 to 65 pounds, and the wording differs: per dog, combined, or "some cabins allow larger."
- Vrbo's help pages say hosts can state pet type and size in house rules. [[9]](https://help.vrbo.com/articles/Add-or-edit-house-rules) The Airbnb pages we read do not mention size or weight. [[11]](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/86)
- A written yes for your dog, with its weight named, is the only approval that counts.

Check any dog's weight and count against a limit with the [will my dog be accepted tool](/tools/will-my-dog-be-accepted). For the broader large-dog picture (stairs, floors, yards), see our [large-dog rental guide](/guides/vacation-rentals-that-welcome-large-dogs); this page is about named managers and the approval message. If two dogs are the issue, see our [guide to how many dogs a rental allows](/guides/how-many-dogs-can-you-bring-to-a-vacation-rental).

## The six managers that state a limit

Quotes are from each manager's public page, read 2026-11-18. Policies change and many vary by home.

| Manager | What the page says | Reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hearthside Cabin Rentals | "no larger than 35 pounds"; "SOME cabins will allow larger pets"; "a one-time fee of $75 for a large pet" [[1]](https://hearthsidecabinrentals.com/frequently-asked-questions) | 35 lb standard, soft limit |
| Cabin Rentals of Georgia | "Pet Friendly Cabins only accept dogs under 35 lbs., non-shedding, and crate trained." [[2]](https://cabin-rentals-of-georgia.com/policies) | Under 35 lb, plus other conditions |
| Brindley Beach Vacations | "up to two dogs, each weighing 60 pounds or less" [[3]](https://www.brindleybeach.com/node/3691) | 60 lb each |
| Elk Springs Resort | "Dogs over 50 pounds are not permitted." [[4]](https://elkspringsresort.com/?p=1899) | Over 50 lb not permitted |
| AMI Locals | "Max of 2 dogs, combined weight ≤ 50 lbs" [[5]](https://www.amilocals.com/faqs) | 50 lb combined |
| Emmons Realty | "a maximum weight of 65lbs per dog" [[6]](https://www.emmonsrealty.com/pages/rental-policies) | 65 lb each |

Two of the six are firm about it: Brindley Beach says "NO EXCEPTIONS" and Emmons Realty says "No exceptions will be made for any part of this policy." A 72-pound dog is over all six. A 60-pound dog clears Brindley Beach and Emmons Realty but not Hearthside, Cabin Rentals of Georgia, Elk Springs or AMI Locals. The cutoff for "large" is wherever each manager puts it.

## The two that say no limit

Sliding Rock Cabins says: "Every cabin welcomes them with no pet fees, breed restrictions, or weight limits." [[7]](https://slidingrockcabins.com/) The page states no size limits that we found.

Hapey Cabin Rentals says: "No weight limits. Great Danes and Chihuahuas are equally welcome." and, on the same page, that its pet-friendly cabins "welcome dogs of every size and breed." [[8]](https://hapeycabinrentals.com/pet-friendly-smoky-mountain-cabin-rentals/) It also asks that dogs be "well behaved, up-to-date on vaccinations, and housebroken."

Two caveats worth holding onto. "No weight limit" does not mean "no rules": Hapey says more than two dogs should be mentioned at booking so they can confirm, so a count rule can still sit beside a weight policy. And a manager's marketing page is not your reservation; the booking terms are.

## What the other 30 managers say

Most pages never mention weight. That could mean no limit, a limit set per home, or a limit that lives only in the listing or the rental agreement. Our data cannot tell which. Brindley Beach's own FAQ shows why the question matters: "Many vacation rentals in the Outer Banks have pet restrictions that include weight limits or breed limitations," and it advises you to "review the individual property listing carefully." [[3]](https://www.brindleybeach.com/node/3691) Vacasa's Mammoth Lakes page similarly says weight limits and dog counts vary by home and should be stated in each listing, without naming a cutoff. [[13]](https://www.vacasa.com/usa/California/Mammoth-Lakes/explore/Dog-friendly/)

So treat silence as a reason to ask, not a green light.

## What does a dog weight limit actually mean?

How it is worded, and what to ask: the same word, "limit," hides very different rules. Use the manager examples above to decode the wording.

| Wording | Real example | What to ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Per dog | Brindley Beach: "each weighing 60 pounds or less"; Emmons: "per dog" | Is the weight a hard number or measured by me? |
| Combined | AMI Locals: "combined weight ≤ 50 lbs" | Two 30-pound dogs fail together; is a one-dog booking the only route? |
| "Under" versus "or less" | Cabin Rentals of Georgia says "under 35 lbs."; Brindley says "60 pounds or less" | Is a dog at exactly the number accepted? |
| Not permitted over a number | Elk Springs: "Dogs over 50 pounds are not permitted." | No wiggle room stated |
| Soft limit | Hearthside: "SOME cabins will allow larger pets" | Which cabins, and what is the extra fee? |
| Bundled with other conditions | Cabin Rentals of Georgia adds "non-shedding, and crate trained" | Does my dog meet every condition, not just weight? |

Hostaway's host guide advises stating size limits numerically and says some hosts set none. [[14]](https://www.hostaway.com/vacation-rental-guide/pet-friendly-hosting/policies/) That is the advice to hosts; many pages still use soft words, so ask for the number in writing.

Breed rules ride separately. Elk Springs, Emmons Realty and Cabin Rentals of Georgia all pair weight with breed or other rules, so a dog under the number can still be turned away. Ask about breed in writing before you book.

## Does Airbnb or Vrbo set a dog weight limit?

Both platforms put the pet policy in the host's house rules, and neither page we read sets a platform-wide weight number.

- **Vrbo:** the predefined "Pets allowed" house rule lets hosts "add information like the pet type and size permitted at your property." [[9]](https://help.vrbo.com/articles/Add-or-edit-house-rules) Vrbo also tells hosts to say "if they can bring their pets, how many, what type of pet, and any size restriction," and says guests "must agree to your house rules before they book." [[10]](https://help.vrbo.com/articles/What-are-House-Rules)
- **Airbnb:** the pet policy is in the listing's House rules, and Airbnb says: "Even if a listing says pets are allowed, always contact the host to confirm." [[11]](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/86) Its host page says guests can state how many pets (0 to 5) they plan to bring, which the host can review, and it describes fee structures; it does not mention size or weight. [[12]](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3623)

The practical point: a "Pets allowed" badge or filter tells you the host accepts animals, not your 72-pound dog. Open the house rules and search for "weight," "pounds" and "size."

## How to ask a host to approve a large dog

GoPetFriendly's tip is to contact the owner by email and get the rules in writing before paying a deposit. [[15]](https://www.gopetfriendly.com/blog/tips-for-booking-a-pet-friendly-vacation-rental/) Do it on the platform or by email, before you book or pay, and be exact. Do not round the weight down.

Copy this and fill the brackets:

"Hello, we are interested in [property or cabin name] for [dates]. We would like to bring one dog: [name], a [breed or 'mixed breed, breed unknown'], currently [weight] pounds as of [date], [age], house-trained and [crate trained / not crate trained]. Your pet policy lists a weight limit of [number]. Could you tell us whether [name] can be approved for this home, whether any extra fee, deposit or condition applies, and whether the approval can be confirmed in writing in this thread? If this home cannot take a dog this size, could you suggest one that can? Thank you."

Why it works: it gives a number, a date, and one clear question, and it leaves the host a way to say yes to a different home. If you have two dogs and the limit is combined, give both weights.

## What to document

- The listing page and house rules as they read the day you booked (screenshot or saved page, with the date).
- The weight, date and source of your dog's weight, such as a vet visit or a home scale reading.
- The host's written reply naming your dog and the limit that applies, kept in the booking thread.
- The total pet charge in writing, including any large-pet fee (Hearthside lists $75). [[1]](https://hearthsidecabinrentals.com/frequently-asked-questions)
- Any conditions attached: crate rules, unattended-pet rules, counts.

Hearthside's page warns that a pet found in a cabin not designated as pet-friendly can bring "a fee of up to $500 for additional cleaning and/or misrepresentation," so do not assume an approved dog can stay anywhere in a manager's inventory.

Service animals are a separate matter: Airbnb says hosts must accept them even if they do not allow pets (with limited health and safety exceptions), and Hostaway's host guide says they cannot be subject to size restrictions. [[11]](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/86) [[14]](https://www.hostaway.com/vacation-rental-guide/pet-friendly-hosting/policies/)

If the answer is no, do not bring the dog anyway: the Hearthside warning above is the kind of penalty an unapproved pet can bring.

## What to buy for a big dog once you are approved

Approval is the hard part. The conditions that come with it, and the size of the dog, decide what is worth buying. Two items follow, each tied to a different condition: a crate if the manager asks for a crate-trained dog or you will leave the dog alone, and a bed if the host limits furniture. Skip any item that solves a problem you do not have.

**If the approval comes with a crate condition: the MidWest iCrate.** Cabin Rentals of Georgia's rule is "crate trained," and our policy table shows a crate condition riding alongside weight. [[2]](https://cabin-rentals-of-georgia.com/policies) The iCrate is sold in seven sizes from 18 to 48 in, with a divider panel and a removable leak-proof pan included. The maker lists a slide-bolt latch and says it folds flat. [[16]](https://www.midwesthomes4pets.com/product/dogs/crates-carriers-kennels/icrate-dog-crate/) It suits a crate-trained dog that will be alone in a home that asks for a crate; skip it if your dog is not crate trained and the host does not ask for one. The page gives no dog-weight guidance, wire gauge or folded dimensions, so size by your dog's measured length and standing height, ask the maker if you are between sizes, and check the folded size against your vehicle. Our [guide to leaving a dog alone in a rental](/guides/can-you-leave-a-dog-alone-in-a-vacation-rental) covers the host rules.

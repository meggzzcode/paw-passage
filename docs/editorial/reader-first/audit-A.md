VCA says...") re-describe the table, and the last line is a meta-caveat: "The AAA page we read gives no guidance on running the engine while stranded, so neither does our table."

- "One dog or several" is four generic bullets labelled "our suggestions, not from a source".
- Table header caveats ("This table is our planning aid, not a veterinary protocol. It leans on...") before every table.

**Voice.** Mostly fine and friendly in the tables. Lecture: "CDC says to take a dog that is not used to the car on a series of short drives, building up the time, and to feed 3 to 4 hours before leaving..." Drop the "Ask your vet" bullet into the checklist as one line. Keep the health-certificate/state-requirements line.

8. "One dog or several": two bullets (slowest dog sets the pace; water, towel and blanket per dog). "Backups": delete; its content is in the table and checklist, but keep the sentence pointing to the RV guide next to the opening.

## Pattern summary

- **Source walk-throughs after the advice.** Almost every guide has paragraphs of "X says... Y says... Z says..." that restate a table or checklist already on the page (road trip, boots, RV, gear, enrichment, first aid). Fix: one clause of attribution in the line itself; sources live in the frontmatter.
- **Maker-spec paragraphs.** Weights in grams, panel counts, size ranges and colour options inside body copy (Ruffwear fleece, iCrate, Pawtect pads, Petmate yard, Baskerville, FDK). Fix: two sentences per product (who it's for, who should skip it); the product card already carries the spec.
- **Duplicated topics across guides.** Boots/paw care appears in the draft boots guide, cold-weather gear and the road trip guide; the towel pick appears in four guides; car-safety pointers are everywhere. Fix: one owner per topic, others get a one-line link (do not link to drafts from published guides until they publish).
- **Dek, opening, "short answer" box and "Next step" restate each other** (RV, Smoky, road trip, car safety). Fix: opening is the answer; delete the box.
- **Meta sections** ("What we could not confirm", "Where each part comes from", "How we counted", "This table is our planning aid...") sit in 6 of 10 guides. Keep the caveat that changes a decision in-line, cut the sections.
- **Bring and Care are buried in rule-heavy guides** (Smoky, puppy, RV): the rules come first and the "what to bring / what to do" arrives last or never. Fix: reorder so Know, Bring and Care each get a visible block.
- **Checklist duplication.** A message and a checklist that carry the same asks (Smoky, puppy); a "Before you leave" that repeats the criteria (car safety, road trip). Fix: one copyable artifact.
- **What works and should be copied:** the packing list's checklist-first layout; the boots guide's surface-by-walk table; the road trip guide's timeline; the puppy guide's host message and the line "you have lost an email, not a booking".
- **Voice:** no invented first-person experience found; the lecture tone comes from attributing every sentence to a source. A friend-voice rewrite of the same sentence without the attribution (and safety wording untouched) is the main voice fix.

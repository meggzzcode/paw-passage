Not legal advice: a lawyer reviews any sale agreement, the policy pages and any trademark question. Nothing here creates a new account or starts a transfer; it is a record.

| Name                                      | Rotate on transfer?           | Consequence                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

## Gumroad: what the official pages say

Read on 2026-11-26 directly from gumroad.com/terms ("Last Updated Date: September 14, 2026"):

- Section 14(viii) prohibits "transfers your account and username to another party without our consent".
- Section 4.4: "You may not share your Account or password with anyone"; Gumroad "reserves the right to remove or reclaim any usernames at any time and for any reason".
- Section 27.2 (Assignment): the Agreement and your rights and obligations may not be assigned, subcontracted, delegated or otherwise transferred without Gumroad's prior written consent; a transfer in violation is "null and void". Section 11.4: unpaid amounts that cannot be transferred are forfeited when a change takes effect.
- Sections 23.3 and 23.4: Gumroad may suspend or terminate accounts in its sole discretion; a terminated seller shall not re-register.

Not read directly: the Gumroad help center pages (https://gumroad.com/help and the article URLs) return only a title or redirect to the index when fetched here, so their text could not be read. A web search surfaced summaries (not the official text) pointing to these articles: license keys (https://gumroad.com/help/article/76-license-keys), sales export (https://gumroad.com/help/article/74-the-analytics-dashboard, https://gumroad.com/help/article/268-customer-dashboard), multiple accounts (https://gumroad.com/help/article/252-multiple-accounts), deleting an account (https://help.gumroad.com/article/37-how-to-delete-your-gumroad-account). One summary of the multiple-accounts article described changing the account email and payout details; that was not confirmed on the page and conflicts with the terms above, so it is UNVERIFIED and must not be followed. Whether keys or products can move between accounts, and how payout switching works, are UNVERIFIED: ask Gumroad support in writing before any sale.

**Future checkout migration (no second payment system is built).** Keep the internal customer identity and entitlement system: customer records are keyed by an internal `cus_` id, found through `purchase/<hash>` where the hash is an HMAC of the provider name and the purchase reference or license code. A new checkout provider would only need to implement the provider interface (`src/server/passport/provider.ts`) and map its purchase references to the existing hashes. Before leaving Gumroad the owner exports, to private storage: the sales and customer list (CSV from the dashboard), every license key with its purchase reference and status, and refunds and chargebacks. Each exported purchase is then mapped to the existing entitlement, or a one-time grandfathering list is created, so existing customers keep access without re-buying. Details and what is stored: `docs/OPERATIONS.md`, customer data section.

## Order of transfer (summary)

Premium backup and handover notes first; then repository, Netlify, secrets (`SESSION_SECRET` new, `PP_DATA_KEY` kept), checkout, domain, analytics and mailbox; the seller removes self from every account last. No customer data goes to a prospective buyer during diligence; share sanitized views only. Policy pages (Privacy, Terms, Refund, Contact, affiliate disclosure) name the operator and must be updated by the new owner.

## Top 5 issues (most important first)

1. **The H1 promises "find" but the page only teaches "vet".** Where: H1 and the section "Find candidates". What is wrong: the page gives no named stay, no link to the site's own stays or filters, and defers the managers with no weight limit to a sibling guide (`vacation-rentals-with-no-dog-weight-limit`). "Find candidates" is four generic bullets (Airbnb options, Vacasa, a Sun Palace filter, Vrbo). A reader searching "large dog friendly vacation rentals" gets the checks but no next place to click. Fix: edits 1 and 2.
2. **Uncited named-business claim.** Where: "Larger dogs are not banned everywhere: Sliding Rock Cabins, for example, says every cabin has no weight limits." What is wrong: it is the only specific business claim without a citation number or a source in the sources block (12 sources; Sliding Rock is not among them), in an article whose Trust line says every limit comes from the manager's own page. Fix: edit 3.
3. **Gear block does not touch the rental problems the article raises, and it interrupts the argument.** Where: "Buying checklist: gear for a big dog" through the four cards, placed between "Five checks before you pay" and "When a breed rule applies". What is wrong: check 3 says polished wood and tile are "slick for a heavy dog" and check 2 is about steps and sleeping level, but all four picks (hammock, cargo cape, ramp, hitch step) are car gear; nothing helps on the rental floor or at the bedroom, and there is no decision rule between ramp, hitch step and hammock. The breed-rule section then resumes after a long gear section. Fix: edits 4 and 5.
4. **Seat-cover pick is easy to misread.** Where: the "Room in the car" paragraph and its "Best for large vehicles" card. What is wrong: the paragraph names no product; the card is a 63 by 56 in "extended width" hammock for full-size trucks and large SUVs, which is too wide for many cars, and the text only says "Match the maker's stated dimensions". The name never appears in prose, so the reader hits a card cold. Fix: edit 6.
5. **Citation order and small reading faults.** Where: gear paragraph "Kurgo's Rover Cargo Cape is 52 inches wide..." cites [12] before the breed section cites [10] and [11]; the section order "How sure we are" before "What to buy first" puts the trust box ahead of the close; "Do not round your dog's weight down" and "get it in writing" are stated in the short answer, decode section, five checks and FAQ (four times). Fix: edits 7 and 8.

Also: the "Decode the wording" table and the weight table are both good on a phone (the second just overflows by 3 px at 353/350, no visible issue); the "See it at Amazon" button is dark green on rust, low contrast; both hammock and ramp products are linked only from their cards, not in prose.

## What works well
- The weight table (six named managers, stated limit, per dog or combined) with the "AMI Locals' 50 pounds is shared" and "a 60-pound dog clears Brindley Beach and is over at Elk Springs" examples is concrete, sourced and exactly what the competitor pages named in the research block lack. The pin description reuses it, which gives the pin a real hook.
- "Permission versus practical fit" is a clear two-test frame, and the sample host message ("currently 82 pounds, a Labrador-mastiff mix... tell us the entrance steps, sleeping-level access, unattended-pet rule and total pet charge") is ready to paste.
- The Insurance Information Institute paragraph keeps the breed-rule discussion honest ("a real limit for some properties and not a universal one") and separates pet health policy from liability cover.

## Buyer walkthrough
1. Lands from "vacation rentals that welcome large dogs", sees the Lab at 80 pounds hook and the short answer; the decision rule (written approval for this dog) is in the first screen.
2. Reads the weight table and decode table, uses the accepted-or-not tool link; best part of the page.
3. Wants candidates; gets platform instructions and a pointer to sibling guides, no named stays or hub link.
4. Reaches the gear block after five checks; four car-only picks; no floor, crate or bed product to match checks 2 and 3.
5. "What to buy first" is short and well ordered (nothing until written yes, measurements, ramp only if needed, seat cover); good close, with tool links.

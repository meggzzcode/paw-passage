# FINAL2 reader review: two-large-dogs-in-one-car

Viewed at 390 and 1280 px. Role: SUV owner with two large dogs, before buying.

## What works
- First screen states it is a layout problem and says plainly "We found no independent crash test of two dogs side by side."
- A clear "What to measure before buying anything" list (floor length/width, wheel-well width, hatch height, rear belt positions, girth, anchors, barrier opening). A reader can name the measurements.
- Safety claims are attributed (ASPCA, CDC, CPS, makers) and the page states limits: harness "Not CPS certified", Ruffwear's 25/45/75 lb mannequin tests, "Above 75 lb we found no harness with a published test". Arithmetic checks out (2 x (70+38) = 216 lb; 2 x 20.5 = 41 in; 2 x (110+45) = 310 lb; 2 x 22 = 44 in).

## Observable defects
1. Audience mismatch in cards: for "two large dogs" the page gives a card "FOR EACH DOG UNDER 70 LB" (Lucky Duck Intermediate) with the heading just "Kennel / Button opens Intermediate" and a chip "CPS lists Large (one dog)" on the Intermediate card, which is confusing. The prose then says "two large dogs will often need two Larges".
2. Off-topic cards: the MidWest iCrate and JOEJOY bed are "for the rental", not the car (the page says "do not use it as the car restraint"). Two of nine cards serve a different question; the second (bed, "listing-only pick, lesser-known brand") has no safety or travel reason.
3. No typical wheel-well/cargo-width figure is given for an SUV, so the reader is told the width limit ("at least 44 inches of width before the walls") but not whether that is likely to fit; "the narrowest point between the arches is usually the real limit" is an unsourced assertion.
4. Repeated answers: "Interior sizes only are published, so ask the maker for exterior" appears twice (measure section and strapped crates); "Kurgo barrier does not fit mini vans" appears twice (sedan and minivan bullets); "a barrier is a divider, not a restraint" appears in the setup list, barrier intro and checklist; "Never share a crate" in body and FAQ.
5. Kurgo Tru-Fit: card chip "Size chart: 5-110 lbs" next to "crash tested to 75 lb" in a guide for large dogs invites misreading, even though the text flags it.
6. Length: about 15,000 px on mobile, 9 product cards, 8 min read; the barrier cards are three near-identical cards.
7. The "Final setup checklist" is plain text, not tickable and not near the top; a reader wanting the measuring list as a checklist must scroll back.

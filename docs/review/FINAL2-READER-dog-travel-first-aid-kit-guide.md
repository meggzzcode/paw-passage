# Reader review: dog-travel-first-aid-kit-guide (390 and 1280 px)

## Defects (observable)
1. Heat advice conflicts with the sibling guide. Here: "towels wetted with room-temperature water around the neck, armpits and groin... Do not put the dog in a tub of cool or cold water". The cooling guide says "Spray or sponge with tepid or cool water" and "wet the dog". Pick one method across the site.
2. Muzzle and heat hazard unaddressed. The kit lists "Gauze roll for wrapping wounds or muzzling an injured animal" and a gauze/triangular-bandage improvised muzzle, and the same page covers heatstroke (a panting dog). Only "Do not muzzle a pet who is vomiting" is stated. No warning never to muzzle a dog that is overheating, struggling to breathe or short-snouted. This could harm.
3. Hydrogen peroxide is listed as a kit item ("Items with conditions attached"), then "Leave out ... Hydrogen peroxide for cleaning wounds", and the Trail Dog card text notes the kit contains peroxide and diphenhydramine. The text does say to settle it with the vet, but a reader sees peroxide in the kit and in the leave-out list. Also diphenhydramine appears with no dose (good) but also no note that these tablets are in a kit the page recommends.
4. Thermometer: "Ask your vet to show you how to take a temperature and what reading should prompt a call." No normal range or the fact that it is rectal; the heat section never connects to the thermometer, and the cooling guide uses 104/105 F. Kit and card agree (card says no thermometer in Trail Dog), but the page does not give the number.
5. Car-kit row says "a cold pack" but cold pack is only under "Also worth carrying", not on the AVMA checklist the page says the list follows. Minor mismatch. The kit table says "Prioritise" (British) in an otherwise US page.
6. Poison control numbers match published numbers (ASPCA 888-426-4435, Pet Poison Helpline 855-764-7661); fee note present. No dose or drug advice given; "We list no doses" is stated. Good.
7. Four product cards at 390 px are long and stacked with the text restating the same facts ("12 oz; 7.5 x 5.3 x 1.5 in" in text and chip). Thermometer card says "unfamiliar brand" and listing-only evidence: honest, but a skeptical reader may ask why recommend it. Cards all carry a "Why we picked it". No broken links, raw markdown or overflow seen.
8. Length: 9 min read and about 14,900 px at 390; the checklist ("Before every trip") duplicates the contacts/records items from the kit list.

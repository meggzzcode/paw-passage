## Mobile traveler

- Tables render legibly at 390 (3 columns, short cells). The "What we could not confirm" table is 2 columns with narrow first-column wrapping ("Whether Airbnb enforces the 14-day damage window strictly" runs 6 lines). It could be two bullets.
- The "Do this first" numbered list is excellent on mobile. Two checkbox-style lists back to back (arrival / checkout / evidence) read fine but duplicate "Gather your evidence" from step 3.
- Link text "Airbnb" appears about 14 times as bare inline citations, often twice in a row: "(Airbnb, Airbnb)". Cramped and hard to tap precisely on a phone; the reader cannot tell which page is which.

## Skeptical reader
- Trust signals are strong: dated, hedged, "Anecdotes only", "One thread, not a rule", "These are drafts, not proven winners". This is slightly over-hedged in places ("a positive outcome is not guaranteed", "Anecdotes only; ask your card issuer"), but mostly honest.
- Weak evidence is leaned on for a legal-money situation: forum thread, The Cool Down (no outcome reported), two Elliott items, Privacy.com (a card company with an interest in chargebacks). The text does flag this.
- Product: "FOR MUDDY ENTRIES: A WASHABLE MAT / Soggy Doggy Microfiber Doormat" appears in an advice stream about damage claims, between "dog hair" and "What to write back". The surrounding text itself says "It does not change how a claim is decided, and it will not help with chewing or stains". That is an honest but unnecessary card: it interrupts the claim-response sequence and has the weakest relevance of any card on these three pages. A skeptic reads it as an ad placed on a distressed reader. Recommend removal or relocation to the end.
- Disclosure: one top notice, plus the sentence "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases", one Amazon button with rel sponsored. No per-card labels. Good. Minor voice mismatch: "we may earn" then "I earn".
- Inline publisher links and a Sources list (15) both present; the sources toggle is collapsed.

## Verdict: READY WITH MINOR FIXES
1. Remove the doormat card, or move it to after the checklist; it interrupts the claim-response path and the text itself says it will not help the claim.
2. Cut repeats of the 24h/14d/60d figures: drop "Confirmed by pages we cite: ..." in the could-not-confirm section; trim step text in "Do this first" so it is not the lede again.
3. Move the Hometime 72h/30-day correction and the Resolution Center 60-day note out of the main flow (to the could-not-confirm section).
4. Fix doubled inline citations like "(Airbnb, Airbnb)"; label which Airbnb page.
5. Convert the 2-column "Open point / Who to ask" table to two bullets.
6. Unify the first-person voice in the disclosure ("we earn" not "I earn") and the date shown (18 vs 24 Nov).

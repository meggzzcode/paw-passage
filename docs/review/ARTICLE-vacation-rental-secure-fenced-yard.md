## Top 5 issues (most important first)

1. **Where:** "...Our [Georgia cabin-vetting guide](/guides/vacation-rental-secure-fenced-yard) applies these checks to North Georgia cabins." (end of "Gated decks, acreage and the filter are not a fenced yard"). **What is wrong:** the link points to this very page, and no Georgia cabin-vetting guide exists in the library (the nearest is dogs-in-georgia-state-park-cabins, which is about state park rules, not vetting). A reader who clicks it reloads the page and concludes the site is broken; it is also a promise of content that is not there. **Fix:** delete the sentence, or relink it to a guide that really exists: `For the rules at one set of Georgia cabins, see [dogs in Georgia state park cabins](/guides/dogs-in-georgia-state-park-cabins).` Prefer deleting, since that guide is about booking rules, not fencing.

2. **Where:** "## If the yard falls short, bring your own boundary" and its three pick cards (all labelled "Our pick"). **What is wrong:** three products with very different jobs get the same label, and the buyer is given no decision: which pen for which dog? The MidWest pen (freestanding, 24 to 48 in designations, ground anchors that need host permission) and the Carlson yard (28 in high, 2 to 2.5 in bar gaps, small or medium dog, attaches to walls, 6-month warranty) are alternatives, but the text only says "In our view, for a small or medium dog on a deck". There is no "who should skip" for the pen overall (a jumper, a climber, a digger, a dog that panics when restricted: the maker itself warns an untrained pet may try to escape and be hurt), and the leash is introduced with "the leash matters more than the pen" with no rule for which dogs it suits. The closing "Next step" does not point back to any product. **Fix:** add a three-line decision lead-in under the heading (see edit 3), change the labels to "For a dog that will stay put", "For a small or medium dog on a deck", "For leashed toilet breaks", and add one skip line per product in the card text.

3. **Where:** "**What most people report:** five fence-checking guides from rental and travel sites, mostly UK, read 2026-11-16 ...: all five skipped service-visit access to the yard." (the "How sure we are" callout). **What is wrong:** the five guides are never named on the page and are not in the Sources block (11 official sources are listed: AKC x2, CPSC, VCA, Airbnb x2, two Ellijay operators, MidWest, Carlson, Ruffwear). A reader cannot check the claim, and it is a statement about competitor content that we cannot support on the page. The research block does name Bolberry Court, Dog Trip Britain, Wagabode, Wood Farm Barns, but it is not shown to readers. **Fix:** either name and link the five pages in the sources (title, publisher, accessedOn 2026-11-16 as in the research block) or reword to a claim we can support: `**What most people report:** nothing here rests on reports; the evidence-pack and gate questions are our reasoning from the sources cited above.`

4. **Where:** repeated passages. "Partially fenced means leash-only unless..." appears in the short answer, in the "Decode the listing" bullets, in the FAQ ("Does partially fenced mean...") and again in the decision table; "invisible fence is not a physical enclosure" appears in the short answer, in its own section and in the FAQ; the pool-yard point appears in the short answer and in its own section. Also footnote numbers appear out of order (first marker on the page is [4] in the short answer, then [1], [2], [5], [6], [7], [8], [3]). **What is wrong:** at about 2,575 words the page is long for the question, and a reader who scans the short answer is told the same thing three more times. **Fix:** keep the short answer as the one-line version and cut the second occurrences: delete the "**Partially fenced**" bullet in "Decode the listing" and fold its remaining words into the next bullet; cut the second sentence in the short answer bullet 4 so the VCA citation sits only in the invisible-fence section; renumber the source list in order of first appearance in the body (the source list order in the .ts file is AKC, AKC, CPSC, VCA, Airbnb... so first citation in the short answer is VCA = 4).

5. **Where:** H1 "How to Find a Vacation Rental With a Truly Secure Fenced Yard" versus `seoTitle` "Is a Rental's Fenced Yard Really Dog-Safe?". **What is wrong:** the primary keyword is "vacation rental secure fenced yard" (research.primaryKeyword) and the related ones are "dog friendly rental fenced yard" and "fully fenced vacation rental"; the title tag drops "vacation", "dog friendly" and "secure". The search result will read as a vague question and may not match the intent "find / filter a rental with a fenced yard". The reader's decision moment (evidence pack, decision table) is also late: "Request a small evidence pack before paying" is the 11th H2. **Fix:** change `seoTitle` to `Fenced Yard Vacation Rental: How to Check It's Dog-Safe` (about 55 chars) and move "## Request a small evidence pack before paying" and "## Make a decision without false precision" above "## Shared yards need a different plan" (i.e. directly after "Give gates their own inspection"), so the action section comes before the special cases.

## What works well
- The dog-behaviour screening matrix ("Your dog's pattern | What to investigate | What should pause the booking") makes fence height conditional on the dog and refuses to give a universal safe height. No competitor does it this way.
- The evidence pack (sketch, tape-measure photos, gate video, sole-use confirmation, pool layout) and the cleaner-and-driveway gate workflow are concrete, actionable and unique; the decision table ends on "Unknown is not the same as acceptable".
- Trust handling is careful: CPSC is flagged as child-drowning guidance, not dog containment; the portable pen paragraphs state "not a fence", "ask the host first about ground anchors", and the leash card says the page gives no strength rating. Affiliate disclosure sits above the first heading; Amazon buttons carry rel="sponsored nofollow".

## Pitch first, and why

1. **North States MyPet Universal Petgate.** The only gate whose maker page I read offering both a no-drill pressure mount and a hardware option, with a wide opening range. It fits three of the named guides (reactive dog, left alone, no fence). Say plainly it is 26 in high and not for stair tops.
2. **MidWest iCrate.** The "crate for rentals" guides already tell readers to follow host crate rules and use a crate the dog accepts. It folds flat and comes with pan and divider. Keep wording to maker specs; the page itself carries customer complaints about latches.
3. **Soggy Doggy Super Shammy plus Doormat.** They complement the existing wipes and travel shower in the damage-charge guide with a dry, washable layer at the door. Low risk, durable, lasting products.

Richell and BearVault are strong but narrower: Richell is heavy and wooden, BearVault only suits bear country and its capacity is sized for a person's food.

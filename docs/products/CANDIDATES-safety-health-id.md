## What to pitch first and why

1. **Pet Medic kit.** It gives the first-aid guide a concrete example of why to count the itemized list, not the headline, and it is the same lesson as the Kurgo RSG "49 pieces vs about 20 lines" note already on the site. Add the guide's reminder to ask a vet about medication items.
2. **The Beacon.** The strongest evidence of the set: a named reviewer label plus clear maker specs, and it fits the New Year's Eve and bolting guides with no medical or behavior claim.
3. **Petcube tracker.** The subscription requirement is stated in the maker's own words, which answers the guide's FAQ directly, and a named reviewer calls out its lower upfront cost. Say "a plan is required" and nothing about plan cost.

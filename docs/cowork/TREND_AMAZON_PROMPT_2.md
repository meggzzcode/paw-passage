- **UNVERIFIED beats guessing.** Never infer an ASIN, size, certification or spec from a similarly named product. - Record the exact variant you read (size, color, count) and whether the ASIN changes when you change the selector.
- Prefer the manufacturer's own page. Paraphrase facts in your own words (three to six bullets); do not paste marketing text. If a product is not sold on amazon.com, write `not sold on Amazon` and mark UNVERIFIED or REJECT (owner rule: Amazon products only).
- Editorial principle (why we are asking): readers want what to know, what to bring and how to care for the dog and make the trip easy. A product only appears if it makes the trip easier, so "who should skip it" matters as much as "why". Do not pick something because it has an affiliate link or is popular.

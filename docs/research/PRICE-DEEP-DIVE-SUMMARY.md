# What can Paws & Passage realistically sell for? (summary of three research reports, 2026-11-24)

Sources: PRICE-DEEP-DIVE-A-sold-prices.md, -B-valuation-methods.md, -C-domain-value.md (same folder). Labels: FACT (published, with a URL in the report), CLAIM (seller or broker marketing), ESTIMATE (a researcher's judgment), ARITHMETIC (our calculation). Flippa's own pages, NameBio, Quiet Light, Indie Hackers and BlackHatWorld returned 403 to automated reads and were not worked around, so the largest real sold-price datasets were not seen.

## Evidence
- Three zero-revenue starter sites SOLD for $1,600 (parenting, 69 articles, 4-year domain), $1,500 (pet, 106 articles, 2-year domain) and $1,700 (home, 56 articles). CLAIM: one broker's marketing guide, no dates. Each had an aged domain and some backlinks, and sold at 50% to 101% of the broker's own valuation. The pet site with the most articles sold for the least.
- Lower anecdotes: $250 Flippa buy-it-now blog (2015 comment), seller claims of $200 to $400 per starter blog, forum summaries of $70 to $600 (secondhand). Promo claims of $2,000 to $5,000 have no verifiable listings.
- Cost-to-rebuild method (CLAIM, broker and blogger): about 2c per word plus $3 per article, $10 domain, $300 build; or $45 to $150 per article. ARITHMETIC for 30 articles and 77k words: about $1,170 to $4,900. These are replacement-cost ceilings, not sale prices; realized prices were about 1.4c to 4c per word.
- Domain alone (ESTIMATE, low confidence): about $250 to $1,000 retail; wholesale to an investor about $0 to $200. A fresh hand-registered brandable usually does not sell. Curated brandable pet names are ASKED at $1,595 to $4,315 (asks, not sales). Appraisal tools are unreliable. The domain has no age, links or traffic.
- Venues: Empire Flippers (needs $2,000 a month net profit for 12 months), Investors Club and similar do not take pre-revenue sites. Realistic: Flippa starter listings, Motion Invest (reported minimum income $20 a month, starter sites about $800 to $1,000 by search summary), or a direct sale. Flippa fees are third-party figures and conflict: listing $15 to $49, success fee 10% under $50k, escrow about $50: net about 83% to 88% of the price.
- Time to sell: Flippa median 15 days for deals under $50k (all types), Motion Invest 35 to 54 days for $6k-plus sites. ESTIMATE: 40% to 60% of starter listings do not sell.
- AI content: one vendor study of 28 Motion Invest sales (Originality.ai, 2025-11-13) found human-written content sold for 39% more. Directional only. Our guides are AI-assisted and disclosed.
- Nothing priced the dataset, tools, 500-test codebase, handover docs or the directory. Trademark: no exact 'Paws & Passage' filing found, but near neighbors exist (Pet Passages, Paws en Route, Pawsport). Not a real TSDR search; not legal advice.

## Our reading
- $1,500 sits at the TOP of the documented range (the three sold comps), and those sites had older domains. It is plausible as an ask, unlikely as the closing price.
- Realistic closing range (ESTIMATE): about $600 to $1,200, most likely $800 to $1,000. Optimistic: $1,500 to $2,000 with a motivated buyer who values the content and dataset. Downside: $300 to $600, or no sale. Confidence: moderate on the order of magnitude, low on the exact figure.
- What would raise it (no source gives a dollar uplift): indexed pages, Search Console impressions history, earned backlinks, a clean analytics history, first affiliate commissions.

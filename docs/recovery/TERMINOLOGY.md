# Product and stay terminology (2026-11-18)

For anyone writing product pages, guides or hub copy. Nothing on this site is personally tested; stays are listed from public listings.

## Allowed
- researched; "researched from the maker's specifications"
- recommended based on specifications and published sources
- the maker states / the maker's page says (claims are the maker's claims)
- closest match to the criteria above; fits the stated size and weight range
- listed with a pet policy we read on the public listing on [date]
- Not confirmed (for any unknown stay or product detail)
- certified by [named body], only if the body's own page says so and it is cited
- Verified (stays only), only after confirmation with the host or an authoritative source

## Banned unless backed by a documented test or source
- tested, we tested, field-tested, tried and true, we used
- trusted, trusted by, vet-approved, expert-approved, recommended by vets
- best, top, #1, top-rated, number one, winner (as a verdict; "best" is fine only inside a quoted title of a source)
- verified (for a stay we have not confirmed), carefully verified, hand-picked, inspected, visited
- crash-tested, chew-proof, escape-proof, waterproof, indestructible, guaranteed (unless the maker states it, attributed to the maker)
- star ratings, scores, prices, "customer favorite", reviews or testimonials
- any claim of ownership, personal use, property visits or owner contact that did not happen

## Disclosure placement
- AI-use statement lives on Editorial Standards only. Do not repeat it on articles or product pages.
- Affiliate notice per the affiliate disclosure page; never create an affiliate link without an approved entry in content/affiliates.yml.

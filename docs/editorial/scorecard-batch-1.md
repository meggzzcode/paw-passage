# Editorial scorecard, batch 1 (6 guides)

Method: Playwright Chromium at 390 px and 1280 px against the frozen build (localhost:4174), plus source read. Word counts are rendered article text (includes tables, FAQ labels, "On this page"). Phone length = document scroll height at 390 px (about 844 px per screen). No real-user testing was done; these are editor judgments.

Cross-article facts (all six): every page opens with the same 5-line trust stack before the answer (reading time, "Last reviewed", "N sources cited", "Affiliate links disclosed", then a full affiliate/Amazon Associate paragraph), then a "not advice / as of date" line. Footer-style "How sure we are", "What we could not confirm / Unknown", and "Sources and how we researched this" repeat the same message at the end. Citation markers render as `<sup class="cite"><a aria-label="Source 5">5</a></sup>` at 11.6 px, tap target about 9 x 15 px (below 44 px), plain digits with no brackets, so they read as stray numbers in the sentence. Multi-cites render as "5, 6, 7". Tables are 544 px wide inside a 350 px wrapper with overflow-x auto and a "Swipe sideways to see all columns" hint. No horizontal page scroll on any page.

## Cross-article patterns
1. Trust language is overkill. Each page tells the reader it is trustworthy roughly 6-24 times (affiliate paragraph at top, "As an Amazon Associate", "not legal advice", "convenience sample", "not a survey", "as of 2026-11-18", How sure we are, Unknowns, Sources). Christmas and cold-weather are worst. Recommend: one line near the top ("Affiliate links; we may earn a commission"), one end-of-article "How we know" block, and delete the rest.
2. Every page is 2,900-4,100 rendered words and 17-23 phone screens for questions that deserve 1,500-2,500. The same fact is said 3-5 times: intro, "short answer" box, section, "What to buy first", "What next?". Duplicated closing blocks are the main padding.
3. Citation markers are tiny unbracketed digits (9 x 15 px tap target, 11.6 px) that read as stray numbers ("belt 5, and Vrbo ... 8"). Use bracketed or visually distinct superscripts with larger hit areas, or group sources at the section end on phone.
4. Opening is meta before help: five trust/meta lines plus an affiliate paragraph sit between the headline and the answer. Move the answer up; shrink the notice.
5. Tables are 544 px in a 350 px frame with a "swipe sideways" hint on all 12 tables; convert to stacked cards on phone.
6. Repeated verbal tics: "ask the host / in writing" (up to 9 times), "we could not confirm", "the pages we read", "convenience sample". It is honest but reads as defensive; state a data limit once.
7. Affiliate fit varies: natural for car restraint, cold gear, crate/gate; forced for pool (towel, "nothing here is for the pool") and damage-charge (mud mats after a legal-money scare). Do not append a gear section to every guide.
8. One article is a .ts file and five are .md; unify the source format.

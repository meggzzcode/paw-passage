What: a reader scanning from the list to the section has to translate; the page also says "8-item" in the heading and "eight checks" in prose. Fix: edits 1 and 2.

3. **Duplicated brand on the crate card.** Where: card eyebrow "MIDWEST HOMES FOR PETS" above the name "MidWest Homes for Pets iCrate Dog Crate". Fix: edit 4 (shorten the catalog name to "iCrate Dog Crate").
4. **Gear block interrupts a booking page.** Where: "What to buy for the lines you flagged" (about 40% of the page, two cards plus a paragraph) sits between "What to ask the host" and the closing lists. What: acceptable since it is rule-led and says "Most stays need none of this", but the last "What to buy first" list then repeats the same three choices in order. Fix: edit 5 (cut the list to the tie-break line and one sentence).
5. **Pet-friendliness claims in two places are the maker's, but only one says so.** Where: "rubber feet that protect rental floors" in the iCrate paragraph. Fix: edit 6 (add "per the maker").

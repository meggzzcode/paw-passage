## Trademark and meta tags: recommendation for airbnb-vs-vrbo-with-a-dog

Clauses (all from https://creator.expediagroup.com/lp/b/affhub/affiliate):
- 12.7: "You must not use any Expedia Marks in metatags on any website that you own or operate." Flat, no exception, and it applies to any site you operate, not only to the page carrying an affiliate link.
- 12.4 / 12.6: no marks in domain names. Not triggered (domain pawsandpassage.com; slug is a path).
- 12.1: licence to display marks is "for the sole purpose of referring Users to the Expedia Site(s)" and per 12.4 in the form Expedia provides.
- 11.1.16 and 11.1.6: do not use marks or names in ways that imply endorsement or association.
- Schedule 6, App. 11: VRBO is a listed mark; trademark notice required when marks are used.

Current state: the brand is in `seoDescription` (md line 4), in `seoTitle` (md line 3) and so in `<title>`, `og:title`, `twitter:title`, `og:description`, `twitter:description`, plus the JSON-LD headline (guides.$slug.tsx:33). The page is thus in breach risk of 12.7 as soon as the Expedia account is active, regardless of whether a Vrbo link appears on that page.

Recommendation (clear):
1. Remove "Vrbo" from the meta description, og:* and twitter:* text now. This is the unambiguous 12.7 case. Suggested: seoDescription "Pet filters, fees, pet counts and service animals on the two biggest rental platforms, and how to compare the real total for two listings." (Check length 120-160.)
2. Remove it from the `<title>` as well, because og:title and twitter:title are meta tags and the title tag is the same string in this codebase (seo.ts:23-32); safest: seoTitle "Dog-Friendly Rental Platforms Compared: Pet Fees" or similar. SEO cost [ESTIMATE]: the exact-match query "Airbnb vs Vrbo with a dog" loses some title match, but Google reads the H1 and body, and the H1 (`title`, md line 2) can keep the names. 12.7 does not name H1 or body.
3. Either keep the H1 and body with factual names plus the App. 11 trademark notice, or, if Expedia replies that body use is restricted, neutralise. Get the answer in writing: email affiliatehubsupport@expediagroup.com (support) and IPMatters@expedia.com (trademark), citing 12.7 and Schedule 6, before the guide is promoted.
4. The JSON-LD headline (`g.title`) is not a `<meta>` tag; leave it, but a cautious owner can give `headline` the neutral seoTitle.
5. Do the same check for any future guide: add a build test that fails if seoTitle, seoDescription or og strings match /vrbo|expedia|hotels\.com|orbitz|travelocity/i. (src/test/ has content tests, e.g. content.test.ts, where this fits.)
6. Do not change the slug unless Expedia objects; 12.4 and 12.6 name domains.
7. airbnb-pet-damage-charge-after-checkout: meta already clean, H1 keeps "Vrbo": acceptable under 12.7 but add it to the test in item 5 for meta only.

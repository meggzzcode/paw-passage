## Observable problems

**Rendering bug (visible to readers)**
- FAQ "Do vacation rentals charge more for large dogs?" shows literal markdown: "the [pet-fee guide](/guides/how-vacation-rental-pet-fees-work) explains the terms." The link is not rendered. Fix this before publishing.

**First screen**
- Title "Large Dog Friendly Rentals and No Weight Limit Options" and dek "Six of 38 managers cap dogs at 35 to 65 pounds and two set no limit. Their wording, plus a message that gets a big dog approved." are specific and good. The opening answer ("Yes, large dog friendly vacation rentals exist, and a few managers say outright that they have no dog weight limit. But a listing that says 'dogs welcome' can still cap weight at 50 pounds.") plus three bullets is a strong answer, but at 390 it sits about 800 px down, below the hero and disclosure box; at 1280 the first screen is title, dek and a large image only.
- Expectation mismatch: a visitor searching "large dog friendly rentals" gets no list of rentals. They get a policy analysis of 38 managers, two named no-limit managers (Sliding Rock Cabins, Hapey Cabin Rentals), and "Where to look" that is generic ("Try the destination plus 'dogs any size vacation rental'..."). The value is real, but the title promises more than the page lists.

**Repeated answers**
- "Large dogs considered means ask, not yes": top bullet ('"Large dogs considered" means ask, not yes'), "Decode the wording", FAQ "What does 'large dogs considered' mean?" Three times.
- Brindley "NO EXCEPTIONS" / Emmons "No exceptions will be made..." in the section after the table, again in the FAQ "Can I ask a host to make an exception".
- The no-limit answer (two of 38, Sliding Rock, Hapey) appears in the dek, opening, its own section and the FAQ; the 60-lb / 72-lb example appears in the table commentary, the Airbnb/Vrbo section and the FAQ.
- Hearthside's $75 fee appears in "What to save", and in the FAQ; "Hearthside ... some cabins" appears in the table, Decode, Where to look and the FAQ (four times).
- "Your next step" re-lists Sliding Rock and Hapey and links to five other guides, which feels like a link dump.
- Message-to-host guidance is given twice: the template and then a paragraph re-explaining it with the "Moose" example.

**Voice**
- Hedging about data: "The 38 managers we read are a convenience sample, not a survey, and the six-of-38 figure is our own count"; "That could mean no limit, a limit set per home, or a limit that lives only in the listing or rental agreement, and our data cannot tell which." Honest but heavy; once is enough.
- Citation placed inside quotation marks: `"add information like the pet type and size permitted at your property (Vrbo)."` and `"well behaved, up-to-date on vaccinations, and housebroken (Hapey Cabin Rentals)."` The parenthetical reads as part of the quote. Also "(Hearthside lists $75) (Hearthside Cabin Rentals)" double citation.
- Otherwise voice is concrete and direct ("do not round the weight down", "If the answer is no, do not bring the dog anyway").

**Table**: the 6-row table ("Manager / What the page says / Counted") is necessary (it is the page's evidence) and readable at 390: three narrow columns, quotes wrap to 3-4 lines, no horizontal scroll, small type. "Sources for this table:" line below is a run of six links; acceptable, but at 390 it is a dense paragraph.

**Product card**: a single Kurgo Loft Wander card under "If the host keeps dogs off the furniture". The reason is thin: it rests on one manager (Brindley Beach) asking guests to keep dogs off furniture, and the paragraph itself says "Skip it if the home provides a bed or your dog already has one that travels." Relevance to the guide's job (weight limits) is low; it reads as an add-on after the real content. Same Kurgo card appears in the senior guide. Card does not interrupt instructions. Paragraph/card spec duplication ("27 x 36 in (Medium) or 36 x 48 in (Large)" in both).

**Disclosure**: one top notice with the Amazon sentence (one Amazon link, so the sentence applies); no per-card label. "we ... I earn" voice mismatch.

**Sources**: inline publisher links in prose; 19 sources collapsed with dates. Primary-source quoting of manager pages is the strongest trust feature in the three guides. Some quotes are exact strings that readers can verify. One caution: Sun Palace and Vacasa, Insurance Information Institute, Kurgo were read 2026-11-16, others 11-18 (harmless).

**Accuracy/safety**: service-animal statements are hedged properly ("Confirm with the platform for your situation") and the intro scopes the page to pet dogs. "Do not plan around carrying a heavy dog up steps" is sound. Insurance paragraph is appropriately general. No broken links.

**Perceived length**: 10 minutes; the 10-question FAQ largely re-answers the body and adds around 600 words.

## Verdict: READY WITH MINOR FIXES
1. Fix the unrendered markdown link in the FAQ ("[pet-fee guide](/guides/how-vacation-rental-pet-fees-work)").
2. Move citations outside quotation marks (Vrbo and Hapey quotes) and remove the double "(Hearthside lists $75) (Hearthside Cabin Rentals)".
3. Cut the FAQ to the questions the body does not answer (breed rules, per-dog vs combined, service animals), or trim answers that restate body text (no-limit, "large dogs considered", exceptions, fees).
4. Shorten the dataset hedging to one statement and shorten "Your next step".
5. Reconsider the Kurgo card here (weak tie to the topic and duplicated in the senior guide) or tie it to the furniture rule with less text.
6. Consider a subtitle or intro line that makes clear the guide explains policies and wording, not a list of rentals; or add the two named no-limit managers higher.
7. Disclosure voice ("we", not "I").

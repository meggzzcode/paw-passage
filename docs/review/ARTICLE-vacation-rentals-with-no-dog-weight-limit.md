## Top 5 issues (most important first)
1. Buying advice is generic and has no decision shortcut (this is the criterion that fails the gate).
   - Where: "## Gear for a big dog once you are approved" (the three bold-lead paragraphs and pick cards).
   - What is wrong: The reader with a 70-pound dog gets three items with no "what to buy first", no "who should skip it", and no size guidance beyond "one of the larger sizes". The crate paragraph says the page "does not state weights", then stops. The bed paragraph is not about a big dog at all ("a big dog takes up a sofa quickly" is the only link). Only the ramp is actually sized to the problem. A skeptical buyer cannot tell which of the three matters.
   - Exact fix: Add a two-sentence decision shortcut under the section heading and a skip line to each item (see edits 4 to 6). Keep every claim within what the catalog states.
2. The first screen does not deliver the answer, and the opening is a story with an invented character.
   - Where: "She is 72 pounds of sweetheart and the listing says 50." and the mobile first screen (390 px shows only the five-line title and dek, then the image).
   - What is wrong: The 6-of-38 / 2-say-none answer only arrives in the second paragraph. "She is 72 pounds of sweetheart" reads as a first-hand anecdote the site cannot support. On a phone the five-line H1 pushes everything, including the "Affiliate links disclosed" line and the answer, below the fold.
   - Exact fix: Edits 1 and 2: rewrite the opening as the answer, and shorten the H1 and dek so the numbers sit in the dek.
3. Title tag and meta description disagree and the title number reads as a negative.
   - Where: seoTitle "Rentals With No Dog Weight Limit: 2 of 38" versus seoDescription "6 of 38 managers state a dog weight limit and two say none."
   - What is wrong: "2 of 38" in the SERP looks like "almost none exist"; the description then leads with a different number (6 of 38). The title also drops "Vacation", the word in the primary keyword ("vacation rentals no dog weight limit"). Both numbers are correct, but the pair is confusing.

# Reader-first edit brief (the edit pass after the three audits)

Owner direction (2026-11-25): readers want what to know, what to bring and how to care for their dog on a trip so everyone is happiest and things are easy. Advice from a friend, not an encyclopedia. The audits (`docs/editorial/reader-first/audit-A.md`, `audit-B.md`, `audit-C.md`) found 4 of 36 guides on target, 21 needing a trim, 10 needing a reframe and 1 a rewrite; the common problems: source walk-throughs wrapped around the advice, method and caveat notes before the advice, made-up worked numbers, the best content (message, plan, checklist) buried at 55 to 90 percent, maker-spec paragraphs repeating the product cards, meta sections ('What we could not confirm'), the same boots/towel/product content duplicated across guides.

## Read first
`docs/editorial/READER_FIRST_BRIEF.md` (the test, voice, what must not be cut) and the audit entries for YOUR guides (each has verdict, exact cuts, target word count, a proposed opening, and 'Do not cut'). The audit entry is the plan: follow it; where you disagree, make the reader-friendlier choice and say so in your log.

## Rules
1. Edit only your assigned guides (listed in your task) and write one log `docs/editorial/reader-first/edit-log-<your-id>.md` (per guide: before and after body words, what was cut/moved/rewritten, safety items kept, anything you declined and why). Do not touch products.yml, affiliates.yml, packing.yml, images, pins, tests (unless a test asserts a deleted sentence: then fix the test minimally and say so), shared docs or other guides. If `git status --porcelain <file>` shows a file you do not own as modified, leave it. Never `git add -A`; commit with `git add <paths>` and `git commit -m ... -- <paths>`; never reset, stash, rebase or amend. Push after each guide or batch: `git fetch origin main && git merge --no-edit origin/main && git push origin HEAD:main` (Netlify builds are stopped, pushing is free; retry 503 with backoff). Commit messages end with 'Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>' and 'Claude-Session: https://claude.ai/code/session_01Wtg1HH8F4yVLgmmRX8QVNV'.
2. FRIEND VOICE: second person, contractions, short sentences, specific and calm, a little warmth, 'here's what I'd do'. NEVER invent personal experience, testing, host confirmation or anecdotes. No jokes in safety, medical, emergency, legal or fee-dispute content.
3. REORDER, then CUT: put the answer and the 'do this' first (the 40 to 90 word friend opening the audit proposes, using only facts already in the guide); bring the best asset (message to copy, plan, checklist, decision table) up near the top; move evidence, method and source detail BELOW the advice and shrink it to one line plus the Sources list; delete paragraphs that summarise a source, restate a rule already stated, or describe a product the card already shows; replace made-up example numbers with a one-line real range from the dataset or remove them; convert long runs of prose into a short checklist or 'do this / skip that' table where it helps; headings become questions or actions. Cutting a third is a good result when the cut text did not change a decision.
4. KEEP, exactly: all safety and legal information (heat, restraint, first aid, emergencies, service-animal rules, refund/fee rights); dataset figures that decide something (number plus 'so what'); the Know / Bring / Care content; `{{pick:...}}` and `{{planner:...}}` syntax and placement logic; frontmatter (sources, products, relatedGuides, research block): do NOT delete or add sources, change URLs, change read dates or `status`; keep inline links only to listed sources; no `[[n]]`; no new facts (you may only remove, reorder, simplify and rephrase what the guide already says and sources support; if a rephrase could change meaning, keep the original wording).
5. Update `description` or `dek` only if the new opening makes them inaccurate (seoTitle <= 60, description 120 to 160); keep slugs and headings anchors that other guides link to (check with grep for `#anchor` links to your guide before renaming a heading).
6. Checks (run after each guide or batch, fix your files): `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bunx vitest run src/test/content.test.ts src/test/internal-guide-links.test.ts`, and before finishing the full `bunx vitest run` (other workers edit other files at the same time: a failure in a file that is not yours is not yours to fix; report it), `bunx eslint <your ts files> --quiet` for TypeScript guides. Word counts: use the site's method (body words) and report before and after.
7. Done: each guide meets its audit target (within about 10 percent), opens with the answer in the first screen, keeps every safety/legal item, and passes the tests. Final reply at most 10 lines: guides edited with before to after words, anything declined, test results, commit hash.

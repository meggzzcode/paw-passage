# SEO research for The Adventure Journal

Researched 2026-11-26 with live web searches and page fetches. No search volumes, keyword difficulty scores or ranking predictions are given: the tools used return result lists only, and no keyword-data tool was available. Competition notes are qualitative and come from the results actually returned. "People Also Ask" boxes and autocomplete suggestions were not visible through the search tool, so none are claimed; secondary queries are taken from the related titles and angles seen in the results and from natural variants, and should be checked in Search Console after launch.

## Per-article research

Common SERP pattern: results are pet-brand and retailer blogs (Rover, Chewy, Dogster, iHeartDogs, AKC, Kinship, PetMD, Care.com, AARP, WebMD), mostly numbered lists of 6-25 ideas, frequently with product links. Few combine ideas with an individual-dog note, a practical-considerations line per idea, or a clear statement of what is free. That combination is the gap used throughout.

### 1. Things to do with your dog at home

- Slug: `things-to-do-with-your-dog-at-home`; title "Things to Do With Your Dog at Home: 6 Easy Ideas"
- Primary: things to do with your dog at home. Secondary: fun things to do with your dog indoors; how to keep your dog entertained at home; dog activities in a small space.
- Intent: informational, find simple low-prep ideas on an ordinary day.
- Observed competition: Kinship/The Wildest ("7 Activities"), MSU Extension, WebMD, AKC, Rover, AARP, Dogster, Homes and Gardens, Langford Vets. Formats: short numbered lists; some lean on exercise, others on mental stimulation. Gap: calm, routine-based ideas with small-space and neighbor notes, plus a clear "dog has a vote" line.
- Angle: everyday short sessions and a daily ritual, not a rainy-day list.
- Collection `home-sweet-home`; CTA context `at-home`; related: rainy-day-dog-activities, activities-to-bond-with-your-dog.

### 2. Rainy day dog activities

- Slug: `rainy-day-dog-activities`; title "Rainy Day Dog Activities: 6 Cozy Ideas for Wet Weather"
- Primary: rainy day dog activities. Secondary: indoor dog activities for bad weather; what to do with your dog when it rains; rainy day dog games.
- Intent: informational, plan a wet day.
- Observed competition: Rover, Dogster (several pages), iHeartDogs (two lists), Kinship, PetSmart, Four Paws, Dogtopia, Farmer's Dog, Great Pet Care. Almost all indoor game lists (puzzle, hide and seek, fetch, playdate, spa). Gap: the wet-walk logistics (dry-off station, paw checks, de-icer, thunder) and cozy-ritual framing.
- Angle: weather-specific logistics plus cozy rituals.
- Collection `rainy-day-club`; CTA `rainy-day`; related: things-to-do-with-your-dog-at-home, activities-for-high-energy-dogs.

### 3. Things to do with your dog this weekend

- Slug: `things-to-do-with-your-dog-this-weekend`; title "Things to Do With Your Dog This Weekend: 6 Ideas"
- Primary: things to do with your dog this weekend. Secondary: weekend activities with your dog; dog friendly weekend ideas; what to do with my dog on Saturday.
- Intent: informational/local-leaning, choose a plan for free time.
- Observed competition: local magazines (Milwaukee Magazine, Washingtonian, Boston.com, Inquirer), Chewy (Thanksgiving weekend, 25 things), BorrowMyDoggy, Dogster seasonal, Ontario SPCA. Many are dated event listings or seasonal. Gap: an evergreen, dog-led weekend plan not tied to a city or season; an explicit "rest day counts" and backup plan.
- Angle: one theme, small plan, backup, rest.
- Collection `the-curious-explorer`; CTA `weekend`; related: doggy-date-ideas, free-things-to-do-with-your-dog.

### 4. Doggy date ideas

- Slug: `doggy-date-ideas`; title "Doggy Date Ideas: 7 Relaxed Dates for You and Your Dog" (seven genuine ideas, per the owner's pin headline "7 Cute Dates to Take Your Dog On").
- Primary: doggy date ideas. Secondary: doggy date night ideas; date ideas with your dog; dog date day at home.
- Intent: informational/inspirational.
- Observed competition: Chewy (11 ideas, long, product links, venue picks), Maryville, AKC, Rover, PetsRadar, KONG, Mad Paws (Feb 3 doggy date night), Wag Walking, many seasonal Valentine's lists. Mixed intent: some mean dating a person who loves dogs. Chewy's page is heavily commercial and venue-led (brewery, ballpark, sledding). Gap: dates centered on the dog's comfort, with at-home options and a "quiet place beats crowded venue" angle.
- Angle: date for two where the dog sets the pace.
- Collection `doggy-date-club`; CTA `doggy-date`; related: things-to-do-with-your-dog-this-weekend, activities-to-bond-with-your-dog.

### 5. Free things to do with your dog

- Slug: `free-things-to-do-with-your-dog`; title "Free Things to Do With Your Dog: 7 No-Spend Ideas"
- Primary: free things to do with your dog. Secondary: cheap things to do with your dog; no cost dog activities; free dog enrichment ideas.
- Intent: informational.
- Observed competition: results for this exact query are general "fun things" lists (Best Friends, Chewy 25 things, Care.com 101, Dogster, Fatherly, Hepper, AOL). Few treat cost honestly; some include camping or paid venues. Gap: honest definition of free (free to do versus fees to reach) and a strict use-what-you-own list.
- Angle: free to complete, hidden-cost notes, and a clear statement that free to do is not free access to the product.
- Collection `adventures-without-spending`; CTA `no-spend`; related: things-to-do-with-your-dog-at-home, things-to-do-with-your-dog-this-weekend.
- Count check (2026-11-26): `content/premium/*.json` has 99 cards, all published, 58 with costMax 0; `content/adventures/*.yml` has 14 published, 12 with costMax 0. That is 70 free-to-do adventures across the combined library on this date. The page deliberately says "a large number" and does not hard-code it. Note `adventures-without-spending` itself has only 1 card tagged to it; do not claim it holds the free ones.

## Overlap check

- Home vs rainy day: home = ordinary-day routines (sniff trail, hide-and-seek, trick of the week, room tour, daily ritual); rainy = weather logistics and cozy set-ups (dry-off station, wet sniff walk, living-room cafe, hallway games, spa hour). The cardboard-puzzle idea appears in both with different framing; acceptable, and both cite ASPCA.
- Free vs home: free is defined by cost and includes outdoor and photo ideas; home is defined by place.
- Weekend vs doggy date: weekend is about sizing a whole free weekend; date is about one comforting outing for two.
- Bond vs senior vs puppy: bond is about trust-building for any dog; senior and puppy add life-stage safety.
- Family vs puppy: family is household supervision; puppy is first-year firsts and vaccination.
- High-energy vs rainy: high-energy is about drive, rainy about weather.

## Top issues (most important first)
1. Where: "A kit for the cabin, the car and the trail" and the "Pick by situation" table. What: the main use case (a week at a cabin) has no product; the only pick is a 12 oz trail kit that the page itself says to skip as a sole cabin kit. Buyer has no choice to make. Fix: either add a cabin-size kit, a basket muzzle and a thermometer to the catalog and give each a card with maker-sourced chips and a skip line (best), or reposition: retitle the H1 "Dog First Aid Kit for Travel: What to Pack and Which Kit to Start With", and state in the dek that this is a checklist with one trail pick.
2. Where: "We researched one ready-made kit, the Adventure Medical Kits Trail Dog" (short answer), "We researched only one ready-made kit, and it is a trail kit" (cabin section), table cell, "is the ready-made one we feature", and the "What to buy first" item 3. What: the same disclosure is made five times. Fix: keep it in the short answer and the Trail Dog paragraph; delete it from the cabin section opener, and shorten the buy-first item to "For the trail, see the Trail Dog card above."
3. Where: "One veterinary-written guide says many pre-made kits lack necessary items." (short-answer bullet), then again in "Ready-made kit or build your own?" and in the FAQ "Is a ready-made dog first-aid kit enough?". What: three statements of one claim. Fix: delete the sentence from the bullet and keep the one in the hybrid section.
4. Where: "Antibiotic ointment you have not cleared with your vet. One guide warns triple antibiotic ointment should not be ingested by cats". What: a cat warning in a dog guide, which blurs the point and adds length. Fix: "Antibiotic ointment you have not cleared with your vet; the Trail Dog lists triple antibiotic packets, so ask your vet before using any."
5. Where: H1 "Dog First Aid Kit for Travel: What to Carry to a Cabin" versus title tag "AVMA-Based Checklist" and a body that is mostly the AVMA list. What: H1 stresses cabin, the product stresses trail, the title stresses AVMA; three framings. Fix: H1 "Dog First Aid Kit for Travel: What to Carry and Which to Buy" (or the reposition in issue 1).

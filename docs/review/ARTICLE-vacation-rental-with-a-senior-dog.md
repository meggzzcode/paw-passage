## Top 5 issues (most important first)

1. **The picks do not solve the problem the article spends most of its length on.** Where: "How do you make slippery floors safer in a rental you cannot change?" which holds the ramp and steps cards. What is wrong: the section is about floors and stairs inside a rental, but the two cards are vehicle-loading gear, and the article itself says "None of these pages says a ramp works for a full indoor flight" and "The makers' pages for these two do not describe them as senior or mobility products". A buyer who reads "Stairs and floors decide most trips" is then sold a car ramp under a floors heading. Meanwhile the catalog record for `kurgo-loft-wander-dog-bed` already lists this guide in `relatedGuides` and the article tells the reader to "bring your own bed" in six places, but never names a bed. Fix: edits 5 and 6.
2. **Four overlapping lists, and the buying close is buried.** Where: "Do this first", "Buying checklist", "What should you pack, and what about the drive?", "What to buy first" (about 65 percent down on mobile, at about y=16,250 of 25,000), "Before you book". What is wrong: the same items (non-slip rugs, washable layers, own bed, ramp) are restated in five places, so a reader cannot tell which list is the one to act on. "What to buy first" is the decision moment and it sits after four forum-sample sections. Fix: edits 1 to 3.
3. **The manager-rules table loses its key column on a phone.** Where: "What do manager rules mean for an older dog?" What is wrong: at 390 px the 4-column table scrolls sideways inside its container (432 px content in 350 px); the fourth column "What it could mean for a senior" is the only part written for this reader and it is cut off with no visible scroll hint. Fix: edit 4.
4. **Pad pick: wrong size variant, puppy-pad source, source not in the sources list.** Where: the "Best washable" card for Pawtect Pads. What is wrong: the approved affiliate link in `content/affiliates.yml` points to the 18x24 in 2-pack (the smallest of four sizes, per its own note) while the text says "in a size that covers the bed"; the "why we picked it" says "Petful names it 'Best washable' among puppy pads", and the cited Petful page is not in the article's sources block; and a puppy-pad listicle is thin support for an incontinence recommendation. Fix: edit 6.
5. **Trust labels and SEO mismatch.** (a) The header chip says "31 official sources", but about 12 of the 31 are forum threads (Tripawds, Mumsnet, Airhosts, MST3K) and the text correctly calls them small non-random samples. (b) The H1 says "Vacation Home" while the primary keyword and the title tag say "vacation rental"; the title tag ends in the vague word "Checks". Fix: edits 7 and 8.

Also: FAQ items 5 and 7 both answer "my senior dog has accidents" and overlap; the host message hard-codes "She" and "her" for the dog; the intro scenario ("Your fourteen-year-old dog hesitates at two steps at home") is fine as an illustration but should not read as a case study.

- "Do this first" at the top gives the decision (call the vet, get photos and written answers, then pay), and the Situation table sends stiff or confused dogs to the vet first without diagnosing anything.
- Forum evidence is labeled by count, source type and independence ("4 of the 17 rental-related forum threads", "Three of the four are from Tripawds... not fully independent"). That is the right way to use anecdotes and it is sustained consistently.

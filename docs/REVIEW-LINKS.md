Run date: 2026-11-17. Read-only review: no source edited, no git. Checked from this container with curl through the agent proxy.

Scope: `src/content/guides/*.ts`, `content/guides/*.md`, `src/content/gear/*.ts`, `src/content/destinations.ts`, `src/content/image-credits.ts`, `src/routes/*.tsx`, `src/components`, `src/config`. 276 unique http(s) strings were extracted. 36 were skipped as non-citations: `example.com`/`*.example`/`*.test` fixtures, `fonts.googleapis.com`, `googletagmanager.com`, `sitemaps.org` and the like. 6 more were test-fixture strings from `src/test` (`x.com/1..3`, `amzn.to/x`, `amazon.com/dp/EXAMPLE`, `a.com`). That leaves 224 real URLs plus 16 Unsplash photo pages (240 in all).

Method: `curl -sS -m 25 -L` with a Mozilla UA. Anything 000, 403, 429, 5xx or 406 was retried with other UAs (`curl/8.5.0`, Firefox, Googlebot). For about 60 pages I fetched the HTML or PDF, stripped it to text, and searched for the specific claim the article attributes to it. Where a quote is given below, it is the matching sentence from the live page.

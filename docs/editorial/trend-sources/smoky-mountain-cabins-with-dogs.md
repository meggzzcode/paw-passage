## Dataset method
Source: public/pet-policies-38-managers.csv (read_on 2026-11-18). Filter region == "Smoky Mountains, TN" gives 7 managers (Hearthside, Aunt Bugs, Bear Camp, Elk Springs, Hapey, Cabins USA, Hemlock Hills). Counts computed by reading the columns: dog_or_pet_limit stated 3/7 (all 2); fee stated 6/7 (per pet or per animal 4, flat no basis 1, $0 1; Hemlock not stated); crate wording 4/7 (must x3 incl. Bear Camp also "recommended"; Hapey recommended). Weight column deliberately NOT published. Two-dog totals: Elk Springs $100, Hearthside $110 (150 if both at the $75 tier), Aunt Bugs $150, Bear Camp $150 + tax. Convenience sample, not a survey.

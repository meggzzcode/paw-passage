## Top 5 issues (most important first)
1. **Reader flow (score 3): the same warning is restated too many times.** Where: "A pen is only as strong as the dog is calm" (short answer), "The manual's own warning applies: a pet not trained", the MidWest watch-outs, "Latches, doors and collars", the first FAQ and "When not to use a portable pen". What is wrong: MidWest's "pet not trained to be restricted may attempt to escape" and "do not leave unattended" appear roughly five to six times, tie-outs get a short-answer bullet, an H2, an FAQ and a "when not to use" bullet, and the "Pick by situation" table plus the "What to buy first" list restate the short answer. At 11 minutes the page reads longer than it is. Fix: state the MidWest escape warning in full once (short answer), once in the MidWest paragraph by reference ("see the warning above") and once in the first FAQ; delete the sentence "The manual's own warning applies: a pet not trained to be restricted may try to escape." from the pen paragraph; cut "We steer away from tie-outs..." from the short-answer bullets and rely on the H2 plus FAQ; remove the bullet "As a tie-out replacement" from "When not to use". Target 9 to 10 minutes.
2. **Short answer contradicts the picks.** Where: "**What to buy:** a freestanding pen at least as tall as your dog can jump, with eight panels that fold flat". What is wrong: the Carlson pick has six panels and 28 in height, the Petmate has eight but is soft-sided, so "eight panels" is a MidWest-only spec presented as a general rule. It also gives no height number to start from, so "at least as tall as your dog can jump" is the only guidance. Fix: replace with "**What to buy:** a freestanding pen taller than your dog can jump (compare the maker's panel height in inches, not a size word), a door and latch you can close one-handed, and feet or stabilizers that protect floors. For a doorway or indoor use, add a pressure-mount gate or a folding crate your dog already accepts."
3. **Large dogs and jumpers have no answer.** Where: the table row "Large dog | Check the maker's stated dog size first | No pen maker we read states a size limit". What is wrong: the page's own table says the buyer should check a limit that the page says no maker publishes; the only tall option is the MidWest 48 in size, never named as the large-dog starting point, and the Carlson 28 in is flagged as modest. A large-dog owner (the biggest at-risk segment) leaves with a non-answer. Fix: in the table row replace the second and third cells with "The tallest size of a rigid pen (MidWest lists 48 in designations)" and "No maker states a dog size or weight limit, so this is a starting point, not a guarantee; consider a crate or a closed room instead". Add to the "What to buy first" item 3: "If your dog is large or jumps, start with the tallest rigid size and practise at home before the trip."
4. **No side-by-side of the four pens.** Where: between "A rigid pen for a dog who tests the walls" and "Crates", and the "Pick by situation" table. What is wrong: height, panels, door, floor protection and anchoring for MidWest, Petmate and Carlson are spread over three paragraphs and a long "What to check" section; the "Pick by situation" table names no products ("Any freestanding pen", "A taller metal pen"), so it does not help a buyer choose between the three cards. Fix: add a table after the intro to "Pens, gates and crates" with columns Pen | Material | Height | Door | Floor | Best for, using only catalog facts: MidWest Exercise Pen with Door, wire, "24 to 48 in size designations", two slide-bolt latches plus safety clip, stabilizers can sit on the bottom, "dog who leans or jumps"; Petmate Precision Pet Soft Side Play Yard, soft mesh, "dimensions listed without units", not stated in the sources, removable washable floor, "calm puppy or small dog"; Carlson Convertible Pet Yard, metal, 28 in, 16.3 in walk-through door with sliding safety lock, not stated, "small or medium dog, deck or doorway". Link each name in the table to its product anchor.
5. **Product names mostly not clickable in prose.** Where: "Petmate's Precision Pet Soft Side Play Yard", "Carlson's Convertible Pet Yard", "North States' MyPet Universal Petgate". What is wrong: only "Exercise Pen with Door" is linked in the pen paragraphs, and the iCrate is mentioned with a citation but no on-site card or link. The cards exist and link correctly (Amazon buttons are `rel="sponsored nofollow"`, "Details and specs" go to /gear/pens-and-gates#<id>), but a reader scanning the prose has nothing to click. Fix: link each first-mention product name to its card details (/gear/pens-and-gates#petmate-precision-pet-soft-side-play-yard, #carlson-convertible-pet-yard, #north-states-mypet-universal-petgate, #midwest-icrate-dog-crate), keeping the citation numbers.

## What works well
- It is the only page in the competitor set that treats host permission, floor protection, ground anchors and "photograph before and after" as buying criteria; the host-question list under "Rental etiquette" is directly usable.
- Specifications are attributed to the maker and every gap is stated ("page gives no folded size", "no weight or dog-size limit is stated"), with a table of open points; no strength claim is invented.
- The stance on tie-outs and unattended pens is clear, sourced to Humane World for Animals, and consistent with "When not to use a portable pen".

## Top issues (most important first)
1. **Answer clarity and SEO: the first body sentence reads as keyword stuffing aimed at the wrong person.**
   Where: "If your dog ran away on vacation and you searched for a lost dog on a trip, search close to where it was last seen right now..."
   What: it tells a panicking reader "you searched for a lost dog on a trip", which is the search query, not their situation, and it follows an invented scene ("The door was open for four seconds...").
   Fix: TEXT: replace the first paragraph with "If your dog ran away or got loose on vacation, search close to where it was last seen right now, then call your microchip registry and your host. Do the steps in order; if you only have time for three, those are steps 1, 5 and 6 below." (delete the four-seconds scene; the H1, dek and this sentence carry the keywords).
2. **Reader flow: the first screen after the dek is hero, disclosure and a collapsed table of contents.**
   Where: between the byline and "If your dog just bolted, start here".
   What: a reader with a loose dog scrolls past a hero photo and a boxed disclosure before the steps.
   Fix: STRUCTURE (template, owner): for this guide, place the disclosure after the first H2's list, or hide the hero below the steps. If not possible, add a first line in the dek: "Steps 1 to 6 are directly below."
3. **Buying advice: the first and most important purchase (the tag) cannot be bought from the page.**
   Where: "What to buy first" item 1 and "The tag".
   What: honest, but the one layer the page calls essential has no product, so the buying path starts with a cheap local errand and the page's four products are all optional.
   Fix: PRODUCT (owner): add one ID tag to the catalog with a spec card, or keep the current wording; no text change needed.
4. **Reader flow: length and repeated claims for an urgent query.**
   Where: "Fireworks and holidays", "How sure we are" (table with 7 rows) and "Preventing it in a place you do not know".
   What: about 4,200 words for a query that needs the first 400; "How sure we are" restates the first-hour confidence the article already labels.
   Fix: STRUCTURE: cut the "How sure we are" table to three rows (registry, tracker coverage, host rules) and set readingMinutes to 13.
5. **Layout: "buy first" wording.**
   Where: "What to buy first" item 3: "the Tractive DOG 6 is the one to buy first".
   What: the heading already says it; the phrase appears twice in one list and then the Petcube line says "buy this one for its size" in the body.
   Fix: TEXT: "If your dog is above 8.8 lbs and already wears a collar you like, the Tractive DOG 6 is the simplest fit."

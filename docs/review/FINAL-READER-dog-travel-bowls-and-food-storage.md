## Observable problems
**First screen (390):** Clear. Title "Dog Travel Bowls and Food Storage: What to Pack First", deck "One collapsible bowl, water from home and kibble kept in its bag cover most trips. Cabins in bear country change only where the food sleeps." answers the query at once. Hero (corgi at an orange collapsible bowl) fits the topic. Single top disclosure with the Amazon sentence ("As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases" is first person singular after "we").

**Repeated answers:** The core recommendation is stated four times:
- deck: "One collapsible bowl, water from home and kibble kept in its bag cover most trips."
- intro: "Most dogs need three things on a trip: a bowl that packs small, water from home and their food in its own bag."
- section "What most travelers actually need": "One collapsible bowl and a spare / Water from home / Food in its original bag"
- closing "Practical checklist": "Collapsible bowl plus a spare / Water from home for the drive and the first night / Food in its original bag, inside a container if you like".
"Original container or bag" is also stated in the FDA list and in "The bag keeps the lot number". "Hand wash only" for Ruffwear and Kibble Carrier appears in prose, card and table. The comparison table repeats each product's decisive spec already in prose and card.

**Padding / stock structure:** "Everything else is a refinement." then "Packing and portion-planning considerations" and "Cleaning and convenience" are generic headings with thin content ("Count the days, multiply by your dog's usual daily portion and add a day or two for delays"). "A bowl in the wash never turns into one borrowed from the host" is awkward. The airbnb-damage and cabin packing cross-links are tangents but short.

**Gaps relative to the first-time searcher's question:** The FAQ includes "What size travel bowl does my dog need?" but the body gives no sizing by dog size; the reader only gets 24 oz vs 4.2 cups products. This is the biggest usefulness hole.

**Tables:** One 3-column table ("Product / Best for / Decisive spec") readable at 390, with no horizontal scroll. Not strictly necessary: it re-lists the six products already described; helpful as a scan aid but repeats. Honest note: "There are no ratings or prices."

**Product cards:** Four cards inline (Kurgo Collaps A Bowl, Ruffwear Quencher, Vittles Vault, and one more via text). Each is preceded by a reason, and none interrupt instructions. But "Why we picked it" re-states chips ("The maker lists one size, Medium, with a 5.5 in opening and 4.2 cups (1 L)"). The Ruffwear card text includes the odd "a waterproof lining labelled Food Contact Safe (the maker's wording, no tester named)" while the prose says shell "water-resistant, not waterproof" and lining "Food Contact Safe": confusing which part is waterproof. After Sources, a separate "Gear for this trip" block shows Kibble Carrier and IRIS container cards, which are the same two products already discussed in the body, so they appear as a duplicate placed after the sources list; the Kibble Carrier is discussed in the body without a card, which is the right place for its card to be. Springland, Gunner, Kurgo Splash Free are mentioned only in prose (fine). No per-card affiliate labels.

**Voice:** Mostly plain and useful ("Pick by how your dog drinks, not by features."). Hedging: "no source we read says how fast kibble spoils in a car"; "None of the maker pages we read claim bear resistance, and an airtight lid is not a rating" (useful, not defensive). Parenthetical publisher citations after nearly every sentence ("(Kurgo)", "(Springland, Springland)") are noisy. "Where the food sleeps" is a nice voice touch.

**Sources:** Inline publisher links (ASPCA, FDA, CDC, NPS, West Virginia DNR, makers); "Show the 15 sources". Fine, but "(Springland, Springland)" looks like an error.

**Facts/safety:** Good: FDA storage rules, 80 F figure, bear-country advice that dog food is scented. Nothing wrong spotted. The checklist items wrap oddly at 390: the second line sits under the checkbox at the left edge ("Water from home for the drive and the / first night"), a small layout glitch visible in the screenshot.

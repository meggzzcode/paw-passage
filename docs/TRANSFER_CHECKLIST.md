# Transfer checklist: Paws & Passage (pawsandpassage.com)

Rebuilt 2026-11-25, in time order. Start with [BUYER_HANDOVER.md](../BUYER_HANDOVER.md), the owner's guide; this file is the seller and buyer task list behind its section 3. Detail for each service: [DNS-AND-TRANSFER.md](./DNS-AND-TRANSFER.md) (domain, DNS, Netlify, GitHub, Pages CMS), [EMAIL-AND-TRANSFER.md](./EMAIL-AND-TRANSFER.md) (mailbox), [SECURITY-AND-RECOVERY.md](./SECURITY-AND-RECOVERY.md) (sign-in security, restore) and [COSTS-AND-ACCOUNTS.md](./COSTS-AND-ACCOUNTS.md).

**Rules for every row.**

- No valuable asset moves until funds are secured by the agreed escrow process.
- Never send passwords, tokens or recovery codes. Use each service's invitation or transfer feature.
- **An invitation sent is not a completed transfer.** A row is done only when the Evidence column exists.
- Status values: OPEN, DONE, BLOCKED, UNVERIFIED. All rows start OPEN. Only the people named in the Evidence column may mark a row DONE.
- Evidence labels: VERIFIED IN ACCOUNT (seen in a seller account on 2026-11-25), OWNER CONFIRMATION, UNVERIFIED, OWNER ACTION REQUIRED.

What is being sold, with counts at commit `3e5923b`: [BUYER_ASSET_INVENTORY.md](./BUYER_ASSET_INVENTORY.md) and [buyer/ROUTE_AND_ASSET_COUNTS.md](./buyer/ROUTE_AND_ASSET_COUNTS.md). Traffic and revenue: none established (domain registered November 16, 2026).

## Notes by area

- **Search Console and analytics.** The seller's property does not transfer unless the seller authorizes access. Create your own, verify the domain and submit `/sitemap.xml`. See [ANALYTICS-SETUP.md](./ANALYTICS-SETUP.md).
- **Pinterest.** An owner decision. Accounts are generally not transferable (UNVERIFIED). Clear the Pinterest address in Business settings if you do not take the account.
- **Affiliate accounts.** None transfer. A configured link is not an approval. See [BUYER_AFFILIATE_TRANSITION.md](./BUYER_AFFILIATE_TRANSITION.md).
- **History.** The repository history contains the seller's author email on some commits. Decide with the seller whether to keep it or hand over a squashed copy.
- **Legal pages.** Privacy, terms, disclosure and about were not professionally reviewed and do not name an owner or jurisdiction. Add your own details and consider a review.
- **Domain move to another registrar later.** Whether a 60-day lock applies after a change of owner is UNVERIFIED. Ask Namecheap in writing.

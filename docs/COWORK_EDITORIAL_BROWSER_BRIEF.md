# Cowork brief: live-browser editorial research and QA for Paws & Passage

Self-contained. Date written: 2026-11-24. Repo: https://github.com/meggzzcode/paw-passage (production branch `main`). Live site: https://www.pawsandpassage.com. Background files (read first): `docs/EDITORIAL_AUDIT.md`, `docs/ARTICLE_SCORECARD.md`, `docs/EDITORIAL_STYLE_GUIDE.md`, `docs/editorial/competitors-pet-travel.md`, `docs/editorial/competitors-editorial-commerce.md`.

## Rules
Read-only research. Do not log in anywhere, buy, subscribe, comment, submit forms, accept cookies beyond what is needed to read, or contact anyone. Respect bot blocks and CAPTCHAs: if blocked, mark BLOCKED and move on; never work around. Record only what you see; write UNVERIFIED for anything you could not see. Never invent traffic, rankings or experiences. Do not edit the repository (the owner will merge your report).
Note: the live site may still show the older build (Netlify builds are stopped). Say which version you saw (look for the spec cards on gear guides; if absent, it is the older build).

## Task 1: Competitor pages that automated tools could not read (desktop and a 390 px phone window)
Sites: BringFido (bringfido.com/blog), Rover (rover.com/blog travel articles), Dogster (dogster.com travel), Wirecutter (nytimes.com/wirecutter, dog travel or crate/harness pieces), The Strategist pet picks, Lonely Planet or Condé Nast Traveler pet-travel articles, The Dyrt pet-friendly camping articles, Vrbo/Airbnb pet-friendly editorial pages. Visit at least 3 articles per site (real URLs). Record per article: approximate length (screens of scroll on the phone window), headline, first two sentences of the intro (quote under 25 words), heading frequency, paragraph size, tables/lists, quick-answer or "our pick" box, table of contents, number and placement of images, how sources are shown (numbers, inline links, footnotes), tone, where and how the affiliate or advertising disclosure appears (quote it), how product or booking buttons are presented, anything that creates friction (popups, ads, cookie walls). Screenshot the first phone screen and one product block per site.

## Task 2: Paws & Passage on a real phone-size window
On the deployed site check these URLs at 390 px wide: /, /guides/how-vacation-rental-pet-fees-work, /guides/dog-car-safety-harness-and-crate-guide, /guides/dog-ramp-or-steps-for-the-car-and-cabin, /guides/dog-travel-bowls-and-food-storage, /guides/if-your-dog-gets-loose-on-a-trip, /guides/vacation-rentals-that-welcome-large-dogs, /gear, /gear/car-safety, one /stays page. For each: (a) the first screen: does the headline and answer show before scrolling past the hero; (b) tap 5 numbered source markers: easy or hard, do they open a new tab; (c) any table: clipped column, swipe hint, usability; (d) every buy button: is a commission label visible next to it; (e) count how many separate "trust" or disclaimer blocks you meet before the first product; (f) any layout glitch (orphan separators, wrapped breadcrumb, overlapping, cut-off text). Screenshots: first screen, one table, one product card per page.

## Task 3: Reader test (expert judgment, not user research)
Take 5 guides (pet fees, car safety, ramp, loose dog, large-dog rentals). Act as a 25 to 35 year old dog owner on a phone: would you click the headline; do you reach the answer in 20 seconds; do you finish, skim or leave; would you click a product link, and why or why not. Give 1 to 10 for appeal and for "feels like a publication, not an affiliate site", with two sentences of evidence each.

## Output format (write back as a single Markdown file `docs/editorial/cowork-browser-results.md` on a new branch `cowork/editorial-results`, or paste to the owner)
1. Version seen and date. 2. Table per task (site or page, URL, findings, screenshot file names). 3. A "Patterns worth adapting" list and a "Friction to avoid" list, each backed by a URL. 4. BLOCKED/UNVERIFIED list. 5. Top 5 changes you would make to Paws & Passage, in order, with one line of evidence each.
Screenshots go to a private folder the owner names; list file names only in the Markdown.

2. **Where:** three conflicting "buy first" statements: pads "the buy-first item if your puppy is not yet reliably house-trained", iCrate "the one to buy first if your puppy is already crate-trained", and "What to buy first" (iCrate first, "If not, buy the pads first"). **What:** the logic is consistent, but a skimmer reads the pad paragraph, the crate paragraph and the list and sees three firsts. **Fix:** change the pad sentence to `The Pawtect Pads are the first buy if your puppy is not yet reliably house-trained.` and the iCrate sentence to `The iCrate is the first buy if your puppy already sleeps in a crate at home.`; open "What to buy first" with `Two cases: a puppy that already uses a crate buys the iCrate first; a puppy that is not house-trained buys the pads first.`
